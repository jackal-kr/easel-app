# 0004 Canvas view

Status: accepted
Depends on: 0001, 0002, 0003

## Why
0001 gave a picture you can pan and zoom with the wheel, but no way to see the zoom, jump to a useful size, see pixel
boundaries, or change the picture's size. This feature adds the view tools of XP Paint (zoom commands, Fit to Window, the
pixel grid, a status bar) and the two ways XP changes the canvas size (drag handles and Attributes), and makes a size
change an undoable edit.

## Requirements
Look and input rules come from `docs/design.md` (header bar View and Image menu buttons; status bar; Pan never changes
the picture). Words used below: the *canvas area* is the scrolled area that shows the picture, without its scrollbars; a
*level* is one of the zoom levels 10, 25, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 600, 800 % (REQ-0001-16).

### Menus
- REQ-0004-01 The header bar shall have an Image menu button and, right after it, a View menu button, each showing a symbolic icon before its label ("Image", "View"), then the primary menu (design.md). Image shall hold one item for now: Attributes… (Ctrl+E); later features add theirs (0012). View shall hold, in order:
  - a zoom row: a Zoom Out button (tooltip "Zoom Out (Ctrl+-)"), the current zoom as `N%` (for example `100%`), and a Zoom In button (tooltip "Zoom In (Ctrl+=)"); Zoom In also answers to Ctrl++ and the numpad plus with Ctrl, Zoom Out to Ctrl+numpad minus;
  - a separator;
  - Normal Size (Ctrl+PageUp), Large Size (Ctrl+PageDown), Fit to Window (Ctrl+0);
  - a separator;
  - Show Grid (Ctrl+G, a check item) and Grid…;
  - a separator;
  - Tool Box, Color Box and Status Bar, check items that are checked while the panel is shown (REQ-0004-27).
  View has no Frame Time item (it is a Preferences setting, 0013) and no Show Thumbnail item (0014 adds it). Each item shows its shortcut. The numpad plus and minus without Ctrl keep their own meaning (brush size, 0006).
- REQ-0004-02 A control that cannot do anything shall be insensitive: Zoom In at 800 %, Zoom Out at 10 %, Normal Size at 100 %, Large Size at 400 %.
- REQ-0004-03 The View and Image actions and their shortcuts work wherever keyboard focus is in the window, and do nothing before the picture exists (REQ-0001-39), while a dialog, the quit prompt or the color chooser is open, and while a mouse button is held for a stroke or a resize drag.

### Zoom
- REQ-0004-04 When the user chooses Zoom In or Zoom Out (the zoom row's buttons or the shortcuts), the app shall move one level in or out, with any tool active; the wheel and the pinch of REQ-0004-08 keep working only in Pan (REQ-0001-17).
- REQ-0004-05 When the user chooses Normal Size, the app shall set the zoom to 100 %; Large Size sets it to 400 %.
- REQ-0004-06 A zoom command from the menu or keyboard shall keep the point at the center of the canvas area still, as far as the view's edges allow (REQ-0001-16 does the same for the pointer). A picture smaller than the canvas area stays at its top-left (REQ-0001-11).
- REQ-0004-07 When the user chooses Fit to Window, the app shall set the zoom to the largest level at which the whole picture fits in the canvas area, comparing device pixels with no scrollbars counted, or to 10 % if no level fits, and show the picture's top-left corner. A picture smaller than the canvas area even at 800 % gets 800 %. The zoom does not follow later window size changes.
- REQ-0004-08 While Pan is active and the user pinches on a touchpad or touch screen, the app shall move one level each time the pinch's scale since the last level change reaches 1.5 (in) or 1/1.5 (out), keeping the pinch center still as far as the edges allow. The scale starts again after each level change, at 800 % and 10 % and when the pinch ends. With any other tool the pinch does nothing. (1.5 is a fixed value of this spec.)

### Status bar
- REQ-0004-09 The window shall have a status bar below the palette bar, across the window's full width, with four fields from the left: the pointer position as `x, y` in picture pixels, the picture size as `W × H`, the zoom as `N%`, and a one-line hint for the active tool. The position is shown while the pointer is over the canvas area, also outside the picture (so it can be negative or beyond the size), and is empty elsewhere. Pan's hint is "Drag to pan. Ctrl+wheel zooms." and Pencil's is "Draw with the foreground color. The secondary button uses the background color." All its texts are translatable (0016).
- REQ-0004-10 Each field shall show a new value within the next frame drawn after it changes. During a resize drag the size field shows the new size; after a cancel it shows the picture's size again. Before the picture exists the position, size and zoom fields are empty.
- REQ-0004-11 The status bar shall never make the window wider: when there is no room, the hint is shortened with "…" at its end. (0009 adds the selection size next to the picture size.)

### Pixel grid
- REQ-0004-12 When the user chooses Show Grid or presses Ctrl+G, the app shall toggle the grid. While it is on, grid lines are drawn over the picture (and its checkerboard, never outside the picture), 1 device pixel wide, hard-edged, grey #808080 at 50 % opacity, at every x and y that is a positive multiple of the cell size in picture pixels and inside the picture; the picture's own edges get no line. The lines are drawn only while one cell is at least 4 device pixels wide on screen (so the 1-pixel grid first shows at 400 %). The check item stays checked while the lines are hidden, and Ctrl+G still toggles. The grid is not part of the picture: it is not saved, not an edit, and does not change the modified mark.
- REQ-0004-13 When the user chooses Grid…, the app shall open a modal dialog "Grid" with the cell size (a whole number from 1 to 1000 picture pixels) and an Axonometric check box, and the buttons Cancel and Apply. Apply is insensitive while the cell size is empty, 0, not a whole number or above 1000. Enter applies, Esc cancels, and Apply never switches the grid on or off.
- REQ-0004-14 While Axonometric is on, the app shall draw the vertical lines as before and, instead of the horizontal lines, two families of diagonal lines at a slope of 1 picture pixel down for every 2 across: the lines through y = x / 2 + k × cell and y = -x / 2 + k × cell for every whole k, hard-edged and clipped to the picture. The 4-device-pixel rule of REQ-0004-12 applies to the cell size.
- REQ-0004-15 The grid starts as the settings file says (syntax as REQ-0001-40): section `[view]`, key `grid` (`on` or `off`, default `off`), key `grid-size` (a whole number 1 to 1000, default 1), key `grid-axonometric` (`on` or `off`, default `off`), and the panel keys `toolbox`, `colorbox` and `statusbar` (`on` or `off`, default `on`; REQ-0004-27). A missing or invalid value means its default, silently; an unparseable file gives every default (REQ-0001-05). Changes made in the window last until the app quits, and the settings file is never rewritten for them. A settings file the app creates also holds `[view]` with the defaults (amends REQ-0001-04 and REQ-0002-19).

### Changing the picture's size
- REQ-0004-16 While the picture exists and a tool other than Pan is active, the app shall show three resize handles just outside the picture: at the middle of its right edge, at the middle of its bottom edge and at its bottom-right corner, each 5 x 5 logical pixels with a hit area of 9 x 9 around its center. They scroll with the picture, the scrollable area includes them, and where hit areas overlap the corner handle wins. Pan hides them, because nothing in Pan mode changes the picture. The primary button on a handle starts a resize; the secondary or middle button on it does nothing.
- REQ-0004-17 While the pointer is over a handle, the cursor shall be the horizontal resize cursor (right handle), the vertical resize cursor (bottom handle) or the diagonal resize cursor (corner handle), instead of the tool's cursor.
- REQ-0004-18 While the user drags a handle with the primary button, the app shall show the new picture's outline: the right handle sets the width, the bottom handle the height, the corner both, each as the pointer's position in picture pixels rounded to the nearest pixel boundary and limited to 1 to 10000. The drag goes on while the pointer is outside the canvas area or the window, and the view does not scroll. On release the app shall resize the picture to the outlined size.
- REQ-0004-19 If the user presses Esc, presses the other mouse button, loses the window's focus, or asks to save or quit during a resize drag, then the app shall cancel it and change nothing. Until the button is released, Undo, Redo, the View and Image actions and the grid shortcut are ignored.
- REQ-0004-20 When the picture grows, the new area shall be filled with the background color current at that moment (a transparent background gives transparent pixels); when it shrinks, the pixels outside the new size are removed; the pixels that stay are unchanged. This holds for handles and for Attributes alike.
- REQ-0004-21 When the user chooses Attributes…, the app shall open a modal dialog "Attributes" with Width and Height in pixels (whole numbers from 1 to 10000, starting at the current size), a Default button, and the buttons Cancel and OK. OK is insensitive while a field is empty, 0, not a whole number or above 10000. Default fills in the size a new picture would get now: the settings file's `width` and `height`, with `auto` resolved as REQ-0001-02 does against the canvas area at that moment. Enter is OK and Esc is Cancel. OK resizes as REQ-0004-20; Cancel changes nothing. No units other than pixels, and no aspect-ratio option.
- REQ-0004-22 A resize that changes the picture's size shall be exactly one undo step (REQ-0002-01). Undoing it restores the previous size and every pixel exactly, including the pixels that were cropped (transparent ones too); redoing it gives the same result as the first time, including the fill color used then, even if the background color has changed since. Steps before and after a resize stay undoable through it, and the modified mark and the saved state follow the history as in REQ-0002-12..14.
- REQ-0004-23 A resize step counts for the memory budget as 4 bytes per pixel it removes plus 64 bytes (pixels it adds need no storage). It is one step for REQ-0002-16 and REQ-0002-17: a step bigger than the whole budget stays as the only step and the older steps are dropped silently.
- REQ-0004-24 A resize to the size the picture already has (a drag that ends where it started, a click on a handle, Attributes with unchanged values) shall add no undo step and change nothing.
- REQ-0004-25 After a resize, undo or redo that changes the size, the zoom shall stay, the scroll position shall be kept as far as the new size allows (as REQ-0002-08), the window shall keep its size, and scrollbars appear or disappear as needed.
- REQ-0004-26 If the memory for the new picture or its undo step cannot be had, then the app shall show an error dialog, heading "Could Not Resize the Picture" and body "There is not enough memory for a picture of W × H pixels." (with the numbers), and change nothing: neither the picture nor the history.

### Panels
- REQ-0004-27 When the user chooses Tool Box, Color Box or Status Bar in the View menu, the app shall hide that panel if it is shown, or show it if it is hidden, at once, and update the check item. Hiding Tool Box hides the tool buttons (the active tool stays); hiding Color Box hides the whole palette bar (indicator, swap button and swatches) while colors still change with Ctrl+Shift+X and strokes still use them; hiding Status Bar hides all its fields. The canvas area takes the freed space. The window's minimum width of REQ-0003-03 applies only while the Color Box is shown. A panel starts shown or hidden as the settings file says (REQ-0004-15) and changes last until the app quits. Showing or hiding a panel is not an edit and changes neither the picture, the zoom nor the modified mark.

## Edge cases
- Text the user types: the Grid and Attributes fields hold empty, 0, negative, above the limit, leading zeros ("007" is 7), spaces around the number (trimmed), and anything non-numeric; the same rules for the settings keys of REQ-0004-15 (REQ-0001-40).
- A rule that changes the user's input after they confirmed it: does not apply (the fields are only valid or not).
- The same action again while its dialog is already open: Grid… and Attributes… cannot both be open, and neither opens twice (REQ-0004-03).
- Key repeat on Ctrl+= or Ctrl+- steps one level per repeat; mashing Ctrl+G toggles each time; double-clicking a handle is two clicks without a drag, so no step (REQ-0004-24).
- Zoom commands during a stroke are ignored until release (REQ-0004-03).
- Resizing to 1 x 1, and to 10000 x 10000 (400 MB for the picture, plus the undo step's pixels when shrinking).
- A resize while zoomed so the new picture is smaller than the canvas area: it sits at the top-left (REQ-0001-11).
- Fit to Window on a picture so large that even 10 % needs scrollbars: 10 %, scrollable (REQ-0004-07).
- The grid with a cell size larger than the picture shows no lines; the grid with the picture transparent is drawn over the checkerboard.
- Pan with the grid on draws it; Pan shows no handles.

## Out of scope
Pan's right-click menu with zoom (0010); a thumbnail window and its View menu item (0014); Frame Time (a Preferences setting, 0013, shown by 0014); custom zoom values and a Custom Zoom dialog; keyboard navigation of the
handles (0015); Stretch, Skew and the other Image menu items (0012); units other than pixels; black-and-white pictures
(0012); remembering the grid or the panels between runs (Preferences, 0013); scrolling beyond what 0001 gives (wheel, Shift+wheel,
scrollbars, Pan drag); the selection size in the status bar (0009).

## Behavior reference
- `docs/reference/new-behaviors.md`: zoom 10 to 800 %; Fit to Window; Ctrl+PageUp / Ctrl+PageDown normal and large size; Grid dialog with cell size and an axonometric grid; the pixel grid from far enough in (XP: 4x); Ctrl+G.
