# 0029 Tool cursors in one style

Status: implemented
Reviewed: spec-reviewer, 2026-11-06
Depends on: 0001, 0004, 0005, 0006, 0007, 0010, 0012, 0013, 0014, 0015, 0017

## Why
Easel draws five cursors of its own (Pencil, Airbrush, Fill, Pick Color, Magnifier). They are larger than the desktop's and colored, next to the desktop theme's grey cursors that the other tools show, and two of them (Fill, Airbrush) are hard to read (JOD named Airbrush, Pencil, Fill and Pick Color on 2026-11-05, and all five on 2026-11-06 from the contact sheet `docs/reference/0029/cursors-before.png`). Nine toolbox icons are thin next to the filled ones, and two (Fill, Gradient) are unclear (`docs/reference/0029/icons-before.png`). This feature gives both one look. A mockup on a picture of random colors (2026-11-06) showed the grey and the accent tints getting lost where the picture has near colors, and black with a white outline readable on any color; JOD chose that for every cursor Easel shows over the canvas (2026-11-06, after the spec was first accepted).

## Requirements
### Cursors
- REQ-0029-01 While the pointer is over the canvas area, the app shall show cursors it draws itself, all in one look: a black figure, a white outline all round it 1 pixel wide at the cursor size 24 and wider in proportion at larger sizes, no shadow and no other color (reference: the black-and-white rows of `docs/reference/0029/cursors-mockup.png`). They are: Pencil a pencil; Airbrush a spray can with its spray; Fill a tipped paint bucket with paint spilling from it; Pick Color a dropper; the Magnifier a magnifying glass; the crosshair (REQ-0010-15 and the tools that use it); the move cursor; the text I-beam; and the four resize cursors, left-right, up-down and the two diagonals (REQ-0004-17, REQ-0015-02). Pan keeps the desktop's own open and closed hand (JOD, 2026-11-07: the drawn hand was dropped). JOD approves the drawn twelve on a sheet before the app uses them; the approved sheet is kept as `docs/reference/0029/cursors.png` and is the reference from then on. This changes the look of REQ-0001-10, REQ-0004-17, REQ-0006-16 (Airbrush), REQ-0007-12, REQ-0010-15, REQ-0012-13, REQ-0013-03, REQ-0015-02 and REQ-0017-01, not which cursor shows where.
- REQ-0029-02 Each cursor's hotspot shall be the point the tool acts on: the Pencil's tip, the center of the Airbrush's spray, the bucket's spilling tip (as REQ-0007-12), the dropper's tip, the center of the glass's lens; the crosshair's center (the pixel a shape starts from, REQ-0010-15); the center of the move cursor, of the I-beam and of each resize cursor. A press acts on the picture pixel under the hotspot, as for every tool; the cursor is not part of the picture and never changes a picture pixel.
- REQ-0029-03 The cursors of REQ-0029-01 shall be the desktop's cursor size (Settings, Accessibility, Cursor Size: 24, 32, 48, 64 or 96; default 24) in logical pixels, never smaller than 24 when a smaller size is set by hand, and drawn from artwork at the device resolution at every integer screen scale (never a scale-1 image enlarged), with the same figure and outline. The hotspot sits at the same fraction of the cursor's width and height at every size, rounded to the nearest pixel. When the cursor size changes while Easel runs, the cursor over the canvas takes the new size at the latest on the next pointer motion, as the theme's cursors do.
- REQ-0029-04 Unchanged by this feature, and so stated: the Brush and Eraser outline cursors (REQ-0006-16), already black and white; the busy cursor (REQ-0005-02), the desktop theme's, which wins over all; which cursor shows where and which wins over a handle, a marker, a selection or a text box (REQ-0004-17, REQ-0012-13, REQ-0013-03, REQ-0015-02, REQ-0017-01); and the desktop's own pointer over everything that is not the canvas area, the font card and the thumbnail included. When the tool changes while the pointer rests on the canvas (a key, or the return after Pick Color, REQ-0007-09), the new tool's cursor shows at once, with no pointer motion, as now.

### Toolbox icons
- REQ-0029-05 (Pointer note, Extra shapes, 2026-11-11: Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle and Ellipse are no longer tool buttons; their icons show them as shapes of Shapes, REQ-0043-16.) The icons of Select, Free-Form Select, Magnifier, Line, Curve, Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle, Ellipse and Polygon shall have the weight of the filled toolbox icons (Pencil, Brush, Eraser): drawn as smooth outlines (2026-11-07, JOD chose option B of three): rendered at 16 pixels, lines are 1.5 pixels wide with smoothed edges and the figure spans at least 12 of the 16 pixels on its longer side. Select keeps a dashed rectangle and Free-Form Select a dashed loop; Rectangle and Rounded Rectangle stay told apart at 16 pixels by corners at least 3 pixels round.
- REQ-0029-06 The Fill icon shall show a tipped paint bucket with paint spilling from it; the Gradient icon a square going from solid at its smaller x to empty at its larger x in bars of one color on whole pixels, inside a frame; each in the weight of REQ-0029-05.
- REQ-0029-07 Every toolbox icon stays a symbolic icon: one color, the text color of the theme, in light and dark alike, as today.

## Edge cases
- A cursor theme other than Yaru or Adwaita (a white or colored theme): Easel's cursors over the canvas stay black with a white outline; only the busy cursor and the pointer off the canvas follow the theme.
- A picture of the same black or white as the cursor: the outline of the other color keeps the figure visible.
- A cursor size above 96, or one set by hand between the offered ones (for example 40): drawn at that size. Below 24: drawn at 24 (REQ-0029-03).
- A fractional screen scale (125 %, 150 %, 175 %): the artwork of the next integer scale, shown by the desktop at the fractional scale; looked at in the fractional-scale check named in `docs/phase2-plan.md`, not a test of this feature.
- The pointer over a handle, a marker, a selection or an open text box, or a file loading: REQ-0029-04's order, unchanged.

## Out of scope
- The busy cursor, the pointer off the canvas area (toolbox, menus, dialogs, the cards), and the Brush and Eraser outlines.
- Accent-colored cursors, an option perhaps for later (`docs/phase2-plan.md`).
- Icons not in REQ-0029-05 and REQ-0029-06 (Pan, Eraser, Pick Color, Pencil, Brush, Airbrush, Text), and the app's own icon.
- Brush options that read at a glance (its own Phase 2 feature).

## Open questions

## Plan
**Approach.** The twelve cursors of REQ-0029-01 become cursors that GDK asks for at the size and scale it needs (`gdk_cursor_new_from_callback`, GTK 4.16, our minimum), in place of today's fixed 32 px textures made once and the theme's named cursors. The callback takes the desktop's cursor size and the screen scale, clamps the size to at least 24, draws the tool's SVG at size × scale device pixels and gives the hotspot scaled from a 24-unit grid. The twelve SVGs are drawn on a 24 × 24 grid, black with a white outline, and shown on a sheet to JOD before `update_cursor` uses them; `update_cursor` and `selection_handle_cursor` name the drawn cursors in place of the theme's names, the busy cursor stays a theme name. The eleven icons are redrawn as SVGs in place. Which cursor wins (`update_cursor`'s order) does not change.

**Files.** `src/ui/cursors/*.svg` (five redrawn, seven new: crosshair, move, text, four resize; grab and grabbing were drawn and dropped, Pan keeps the theme's); `src/ui/canvas.c` (the callback cursors, replacing `pencil_cursor`, `airbrush_cursor` and `texture_cursor`); `src/core/cursor-shape.{c,h}` (the size and hotspot rule, pure); `src/ui/icons/scalable/actions/easel-tool-*-symbolic.svg` (eleven redrawn, made by `tools/icon-outlines.py` as filled outlines); `tests/core/test-cursor.c`, `tests/ui/test-window.c`, `tests/meson.build`; `tools/cursor-sheet.py` (the contact sheet: the app's own cursors as the ui test program dumps them, on light, dark and random colors).

**Interfaces.**
- core: `EaselToolCursor easel_tool_cursor_size (int desktop_size, double scale, int hot_x24, int hot_y24)` gives the logical size (`MAX (desktop_size, 24)`), the device size (`ceil (size × scale)`) and the hotspot in logical pixels, the pixel holding the scaled center of the 24-grid pixel (`floor ((h + 0.5) × size / 24)`), always inside the image (REQ-0029-02, -03).
- ui: `easel_canvas_tool_cursor_art (EaselTool, int desktop_size, double scale, int *hot_x, int *hot_y)` returns the `GdkTexture` the callback returns; tests and the sheet call it, as GDK does.
- The hotspots, on the 24 grid, in one table in `canvas.c` beside the SVG names.

**What grows.** One texture per call; GDK keeps the cursor, not us. No cache (P5): a probe in T001 counts the calls per motion.

**Constitution.** P7: the cursor draws nothing on the picture, the acting pixel is still the one under the hotspot; layering: the size and hotspot rule is in `src/core`; P5: no cache; P6: the cursor size is the desktop's setting, not ours.

**Risks.**
- R1 `gdk_cursor_new_from_callback`: whether the size it is given is the desktop's cursor size, whether width, height and hotspot are logical and the texture in device pixels, how often it is called, and whether a size change in Settings calls it again. Probed in T001 (a throwaway window, the callback logging its arguments, sizes 24 and 48) before the code relies on it. Fallback: read `gtk-cursor-theme-size` from `GtkSettings` and remake texture cursors on its `notify`. **Probe, 2026-11-06** (JOD's session, GNOME Shell 51, scale 1; the size changed by `gsettings` while the window ran, JOD's pointer over it): the callback got `size=24 scale=1.00`, then `size=48` within the next motions after the change to 48 (`gtk-cursor-theme-size` notified first), then 24 again; it is called a few times per entry of the pointer, so a texture is drawn per call (6, 3 and 8 calls in the three phases). Width, height and hotspot are logical, the texture in device pixels (`size × scale`). Scale 2 was not on this screen: the scale 2 ui run checks the art sizes, M2 the rest.
- R2 Drawing an SVG at any size: `gdk_pixbuf_new_from_resource_at_scale` (the librsvg loader of gdk-pixbuf, already used for files) into a memory texture, with no deprecated GTK call (warnings are bugs). Probed in T001, also that the blur of the shadow (an SVG filter) renders through that loader; fallback, a shadow drawn as an offset grey shape. **Probe, 2026-11-06:** an SVG with `feGaussianBlur` drawn by `gdk_pixbuf_new_from_file_at_scale` at 24 and 96: the shadow's alpha falls smoothly (86 to 1 over 12 device pixels at 96), so the filter renders; into `gdk_memory_texture_new` (R8G8B8A8) with no deprecated call and no warning.
- R3 A fractional screen scale gives a fractional `scale`: the device size rounds up (`ceil`); seen in the fractional-scale check, not tested here.
- R4 Tests that read a cursor by its theme name (`gdk_cursor_get_name`) no longer can: the drawn cursors have no name. T004 gives the canvas a way to ask which drawn cursor it shows (an enum), and every such assert moves to it.
