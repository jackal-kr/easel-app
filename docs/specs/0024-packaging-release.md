# 0024 Packaging and release

Status: accepted
Reviewed: spec-reviewer, 2026-11-05
Depends on: every feature of Phase 1 (0001 to 0023, 0025 to 0027), and 0028 apart from its T006

## Why
Easel runs only from a build folder: it has no icon, no entry in the app grid, nothing a person can install. This feature makes it installable and prepares a first alpha, so that people can try it and report what they find. It is a release of work still open: the translations are unread drafts and the walkthrough is not yet walked, and the release says so.

## Requirements
### The release
- REQ-0024-01 The first release shall be named `0.1.0-alpha.1`, shown in About Easel and in the tag. The deb and the software-center metadata use `0.1.0~alpha.1`, which sorts before `0.1.0` in both tools (a probe, 2026-11-05: `0.1.0-alpha.1` sorts after it in AppStream).
- REQ-0024-02 The release shall have two packages, for x86-64: a Flatpak bundle on the GNOME 51 runtime from Flathub, and a deb for Ubuntu 26.10. AppImage moves to a later release (JOD, 2026-11-05).
- REQ-0024-03 The release shall be a GitHub pre-release, tag `v0.1.0-alpha.1`, of `github.com/jackal-kr/easel-app`, with the two packages and `SHA256SUMS`, which covers exactly those two under their published names; the deb's file name has no `~` (`easel_0.1.0-alpha.1_amd64.deb`). The tagged commit holds the version and the metadata's release entry, dated by that commit. The notes are written on the release, not in the tree, after M1, and say how to install and remove each package word for word, that the Flatpak needs Flathub for its runtime, that this is an alpha, that the translations are unreviewed drafts as of this release, that the walkthrough and two probes are not done, how to report a problem, that AI wrote the code, the translations and the icon and what JOD has reviewed (S9), and that the checksums catch a damaged download and not a tampered one. The tag stays local until JOD's word: until then a fix makes a new commit and the tag moves; once pushed it never moves. Ready means the packages are built from the tag, M1 has passed and the notes are drafted. Publishing, and making the repository public with issues open before it, are done only at JOD's word at that moment (JOD, 2026-11-05). Flathub comes later.
- REQ-0024-04 The minimum stays the constitution's: libadwaita 1.8, with what it needs of GTK, as the build declares it. The versions tested are named in the notes: GTK 4.24.1 and libadwaita 1.10.0 on the deb's Ubuntu, and the GNOME 51 runtime's in the Flatpak, whose Pango differs by a patch level. The popover's scroll guard of REQ-0017-14 stays, with its accepted mutant, as the minimum is older than 4.24 (chosen while JOD was away: raising the minimum needs an ADR and is his).

### On the desktop
- REQ-0024-05 Installed by either package, the app shall appear in the app grid as "Easel" with its icon and a one-line description in the session's language where a translation exists, in the category Graphics, with search words for paint and drawing, translated with the English ones kept. Its window shall show as that app in the Shell.
- REQ-0024-06 The icon shall be a GNOME-style color icon of an easel with a small palette, with a one-color symbolic form, drawn for this project and shown to JOD before the tag (JOD, 2026-11-05).
- REQ-0024-07 The app shall be offered by the desktop to open `image/png`, `image/jpeg`, `image/bmp`, `image/tiff`, `image/webp` and `image/gif`; installing makes it the default for none. Opened that way with no Easel running, a file behaves as REQ-0005-15 says. With Easel already running, the window comes to the front and the file is not opened, as REQ-0005-15 says today, and nothing tells the person (chosen while JOD was away, as changing it is a rule of 0005 and his).
- REQ-0024-08 Each package shall carry software-center metadata with the app's name, summary, description, license (GPL-3.0-or-later), site, developer, content rating, its release and a screenshot in the tagged commit, whose address is pinned to the tag; and the summary and description in each of the eleven translations, unreviewed as REQ-0023-07 says. The desktop's validators shall accept the desktop entry and, with no network, the metadata, with no error or warning; infos and pedantic notes do not count.

### What installing must not change
- REQ-0024-09 Installed, the app shall behave as from the build folder: the twelve languages and their Help pages are found (REQ-0023-05, REQ-0023-09), and every format of REQ-0005-06 and REQ-0005-10 opens and saves, in both packages. The settings and the palette are in the user's config folder: `~/.config/easel` for the deb, `~/.var/app/io.github.jackal_kr.Easel/config/easel` for the Flatpak. A Flatpak install does not read the deb's and starts from defaults; nothing is moved; the notes give both paths and say to copy the folder to keep them.
- REQ-0024-10 The Flatpak shall ask only for a display, its graphics acceleration, and printing and files through the desktop's portals, with no home folder and no network. A file chosen in Open, Paste From, Save As and Copy To is read or written; Save after Open or Open With writes that file. A plain path given to `flatpak run` gives the error of REQ-0005-09, and the notes give the file-forwarding form. Help shall open the page in the interface's language in a browser, a question from the portal about which app to use being accepted; if it cannot, the release waits for JOD. Print gives the print dialog; with no Picture tab it uses Sharp and Fit on one page, as 0026 says, and the notes say so. A copied picture and a copied file paste as 0016 says, as far as the portal gives them. The app shall write only the name the dialog returned, and never report a save that did not reach the chosen file: under Flatpak, when REQ-0024-12 or REQ-0005-10 would change the name (`a`, or `Untitled.png` with JPEG chosen), the save dialog opens again suggesting the changed name, and nothing is written until the dialog returns a name that stays (a probe, 2026-11-05: a sibling name written in the sandbox never reaches the host; chosen while JOD was away).
- REQ-0024-11 Removing by the commands in the notes (`apt remove`, `flatpak uninstall`) shall take away the app and leave the user's pictures and settings. The notes warn that `--delete-data` removes the settings, and give the step to remove an unused runtime. The deb names what it needs, and `sudo apt install ./<file>` on a system without it is refused by apt.

### Choosing a format in Save As
- REQ-0024-12 (JOD, 2026-11-05, as in his reference, a file type list at the bottom of the dialog.) The save dialogs of Save As (REQ-0005-12) and Copy To (REQ-0016-09) shall have a file type list: "By extension", chosen when the dialog opens, then "BMP (*.bmp)", "JPEG (*.jpg, *.jpeg)", "PNG (*.png)", "TIFF (*.tif, *.tiff)" and "WebP (*.webp)". By extension is the rule of REQ-0005-10 unchanged. A chosen format decides what is written and the name follows it: a name with no extension gets the format's first extension; a name with an extension of another format that can be saved has it replaced (`Untitled.png` with JPEG gives `Untitled.jpg`); a name already with one of the format's extensions stays (`a.jpeg`); any other last part stays in the name and the extension is added (`a.xyz` gives `a.xyz.jpg`). When the name was changed so, a file of that name is replaced only after the question of REQ-0001-42, as the system's dialog asked about the other name. Each entry shows files by extension in any case; By extension shows all five formats' names. Choosing an entry does not change the name in the dialog. A file the list hides can still be replaced by typing its name, with the same question. If the system's dialog reports no choice, By extension applies. The labels are in every language (REQ-0023-10), the formats' names not translated, and the Help page gains a part "Saving", after Preferences, in every language, which says this and that the list can hide files.

## Edge cases
- The release goes out before the walkthrough, the phone-photo check and the fractional scale are done (JOD, 2026-11-05); the notes name them as not yet checked.
- A Flatpak and a deb side by side show one entry, the Flatpak's, and a start of either while the other runs brings the running one to the front. Each has its own settings. Not prevented; the notes say to install one.
- The deb is built and tested on Ubuntu 26.10. By its dependencies (libadwaita 1.8, glibc 2.38) it may install on Ubuntu 26.04, untried; the notes point any other system to the Flatpak. The constitution still names Ubuntu 26.04 as the base, which JOD may want to correct.
- Tested on Ubuntu 26.10 with GNOME; the notes say so. Another desktop's dialog may add an extension of its own; the name it returns decides.
- Dragging a file onto the window and recent files stay out of scope in both packages, as in 0005 and 0016.
- Under Flatpak the dialog may start where the desktop chooses, not in the picture's folder; the suggested name holds.
- A new alpha means installing the new bundle again, as there is no repository.

- (Note, Remove background, 2026-11-09, JOD.) Remove background's model (ADR-0006, IS-Net, 179 MB) and its runtime may ship as a separate download or a Flatpak extension, to keep the base app small; decided at this release, together with the permission for the weights (`docs/reference/0035/ask-dis-authors.md`). Without them the app builds and runs, the item greyed (REQ-0035-10).

## Out of scope
- AppImage; Flathub; other architectures; a package repository or automatic updates; signing beyond the checksums; a website; a build in CI (`docs/engineering.md`).

## Open questions

## Plan
**Approach.** The app gains what a desktop needs (icon, desktop entry, metadata), the two packages are thin wrappers around `meson install`, and the release stays a manual, gated step. The save dialog moves from `GtkFileDialog`, which does not report the chosen filter, to `GtkFileChooserNative`, which does (a probe, 2026-11-05: BMP chosen, PNG reported by the first; JPEG chosen and reported by the second), behind one small helper with its deprecation warning silenced there alone.

**Files.** `src/core/format.{c,h}` (`easel_format_name_for`, pure); `src/ui/window.{c,h}` (the chooser, the second opening under Flatpak); `data/` (icons, `.desktop.in`, `.metainfo.xml.in`, a screenshot, `meson.build`); `po/` and `data/help/`; `build-aux/flatpak/io.github.jackal_kr.Easel.yml`; `debian/`; `docs/release-notes-0.1.0-alpha.1.md`, a draft outside the tag's duties; `docs/engineering.md`, Packaging layout.

**Interfaces.** `bool easel_format_name_for (const char *path, EaselFormat format, char **named)`; `void easel_window_save_chosen_as (win, path, format)` and `void easel_window_copy_to_chosen_as (win, path, format)`, what happens after the dialog; a mark of the sandbox, the file `/.flatpak-info`, or `EASEL_TEST_SANDBOX` in a test.

**Flatpak.** The GNOME 51 runtime; locales not split off, or a bundle would lose them; no test run in the build.

**Risks.** R1 The portal's dialog reports the filter: JOD's run of the probe, and M1. R2 `GtkFileChooserNative` is deprecated: one helper to change. R3 Help and Print under the portal: M1, and the release waits on Help. R4 The saver for WebP and TIFF in the runtime: T006 reads it inside the sandbox before M1. R5 The deb's install needs root, and a fresh machine: built and inspected here, installed by JOD at M1.

**Scenario map.** AS-1 `/0024/as1_version` + M1; AS-2, AS-5, AS-6, AS-10 M1; AS-3 M2; AS-4 `ui-0017` + M1; AS-7 tests `desktop-entry`, `metainfo`, `translations`; AS-8 test `install-run` + M1; AS-9 test `flatpak-permissions`, `/0024/as9_dialog_opens_again` + M1; AS-11 core `as11_name_for`, `/0024/as11_file_type_list`, `/0024/as11_format_decides`, `translations` + M1.

Constitution: P6 and P7 untouched. The constitution's "Ubuntu 26.04" and the machine's 26.10 are JOD's to reconcile.
