# 0017 Text

Status: implemented
Depends on: 0001 (settings file), 0003 (colors), 0006 (toolbox and options panel), 0013 (selection: put-down events, mode buttons, context menu), 0015 (handles), 0016 (clipboard)

## Why
People annotate screenshots and pictures with a few words: a label, an arrow's caption, a date. XP Paint's Text tool does this with a
box typed into on the picture and put down as pixels; Easel keeps that workflow and adds what XP lacked: formatting that can differ
within one box, and text that keeps its formatting when copied to or from other apps.

## Requirements

### Terms
- REQ-0017-26 A text box ("the box") is a rectangle of the picture that holds text. Its left, top and width are set when it is made
  (REQ-0017-03) and changed by a move (REQ-0017-09) or a resize (REQ-0017-10); its "set height" likewise. Its height is the larger of
  the set height and the height of its text: it grows toward larger y as lines are added and shrinks back, never below the set height,
  as lines go. The outline, the move band, the handles, the Opaque fill and the put-down all use the box as it is at that
  moment. The style at the caret is the style of the character before the caret (at the start of the text or of a line started by Enter, of the character after it;
  at the start of a wrapped line, of the character before it, as anywhere else; in an empty box, the box's starting style,
  REQ-0017-12; JOD, 2026-10-26). The size is in points at 96 pixels per inch: a size of 12 is a font em of 16
  picture pixels; a line is as tall as the font says for its tallest text.

### The tool
- REQ-0017-01 The toolbox shall show Text, a symbolic "A" in the toolbox's style with the tooltip "Text" (JOD, 2026-10-26), after
  Airbrush in the final order of REQ-0006-01. The status bar hint (REQ-0004-09) shall be "Inserts text into the picture." While Text is
  active the cursor over the canvas area shall be the crosshair of REQ-0010-15; over the inside of an open box the text cursor (I-beam);
  over its move band (REQ-0017-09) the move cursor; over one of its handles that handle's resize cursor (REQ-0015-02). While a box is
  dragged out or open, the status bar shows its size as "W × H" as REQ-0013-06 does for a selection. Choosing Text while it is active
  changes nothing.
- REQ-0017-02 While Text is active, the options panel (REQ-0006-02) shall show two mode buttons drawn exactly as Select's
  (REQ-0013-02), one above the other: Opaque (a filled square) and Transparent (a dashed square outline), the chosen one highlighted,
  with the tooltips "Opaque: fill the text box with the background color" and "Transparent: only the text". Transparent is chosen at
  each start (JOD, 2026-10-26); the mode is kept while the app runs, is the Text tool's own (Select's and the Eraser's are separate),
  and is not a setting (as Select's mode, REQ-0013-02: a mode is not a default; JOD, 2026-10-26, logged as a deliberate exception to
  P6). Changing it while a box is open changes that box at once. The panel holds
  nothing else: no font button (the font card is the only font control, REQ-0017-14; JOD, 2026-10-26) and no Smooth button (Phase 2).

### Making a box
- REQ-0017-03 When the user presses the primary button with Text in the canvas area, with no box open, and drags the pointer beyond the
  desktop's drag threshold (8 logical pixels on the screen by default, whatever the zoom), the app shall show the rectangle being
  dragged out with the box's outline (REQ-0017-05), from the press's pixel to the pointer's, in any direction, kept inside the picture
  (a pointer outside it counts as the nearest pixel of the picture's edge); once the pointer has gone beyond the threshold during a
  press, that press is a drag to its end, also if the pointer comes back within the threshold. At the release a drag becomes an open
  box with that rectangle's left, top, width and set height, but at least one line of the starting style high: a shorter rectangle
  grows toward larger y, and moves toward smaller y as far as the picture's bottom edge requires. A press and release with the pointer
  kept within the drag threshold of the press the whole time (a click, also when the hand moves a little; at a low zoom such a click
  may cover several picture pixels; JOD, 2026-10-26) shall make a box whose top-left pixel is the press's pixel, as wide as twenty
  times the width of the digit 0 in the starting style and one line high, its width cut at the picture's right edge
  and its place moved toward smaller y as a short drag's is (JOD, 2026-10-26: later Paint versions do this; XP needs a drag). A box
  is never more than the picture's height: on a picture shorter than one line, it is as tall as the picture (REQ-0017-07). A press with
  a box open does what REQ-0017-17 says and makes no box.
- REQ-0017-04 A press with Text shall be ignored when a press of a shape tool is (REQ-0010-17); the middle button does nothing. During
  the drag of REQ-0017-03, the other button, Esc or Undo shall cancel it with no box made (as REQ-0013-18); Enter, Delete, Select All,
  Redo, the arrow keys and typed characters do nothing; a release that never arrives or a lost focus ends the drag as REQ-0013-18 says
  (REQ-0007-18), and a press that shows the lost release acts as a press of its own. Tool changes, the View and Image actions and the
  file actions do nothing while the button is held (REQ-0001-09, REQ-0004-03). The rectangle is kept in picture pixels, so scrolling
  during the drag, or a window resize at the fit zoom, does not change it (as REQ-0015-03).
- REQ-0017-05 While a box is open it shall be outlined by the selection's dashed line of REQ-0013-05 (black and white dashes) with
  the thickness and dash length of REQ-0014-17 (a): 1 logical pixel thick up to 400 %, 2 from 600 %; standing still, with no tint and
  no moving dashes (JOD, 2026-10-26, replacing the earlier "as a selection");
  and show the eight navy handles of REQ-0015-01 round the box; all over the picture and not part of it. Its text shall be shown as
  exactly the pixels the put-down will write (REQ-0017-16), at every zoom; the selected text has the accent color behind it; the caret,
  at the place of the next character, is a vertical line in the color of the style at the caret, as tall as its line's text, 1
  logical pixel wide below 400 % and 2 from 400 %, blinking as the desktop's setting says. In Opaque mode the box shows filled with the background color under the text; in Transparent
  mode the picture shows through it. The box scrolls and zooms with the picture.

### Typing
- REQ-0017-06 While a box is open, keys shall go to it wherever the focus is in the window, except into the family and size fields of
  the font card (REQ-0017-14) and into dialogs, as in other GNOME text fields: input methods, compose and dead keys work, and
  right-to-left scripts are laid out right to left. Enter and the keypad's Enter start a new line; Numpad + and − type "+" and "−"
  (they do not change a size, REQ-0007-14); Space and Enter on a focused button of the toolbox or the options panel go to the box too.
  Backspace and Delete remove a character, or the selected text; the arrow keys, Home and End move the caret (Ctrl with them by words,
  or to the start and end of the text), Shift with them selects; a primary press inside the box places the caret, a drag selects, a
  double-click selects a word. Lines wrap at the box's width, between words where they can and inside a word longer than the width
  (a box 1 pixel wide holds one character a line). Emoji and other color glyphs are drawn in their own colors, hard-edged: a pixel
  takes the glyph's color where the glyph covers at least half of it, as for letters (REQ-0017-16), and keeps its own otherwise; they
  ignore the text color, a color pick does not recolor them, and the size and the box's mode apply to them as to letters (JOD,
  2026-10-26, after the probe of T001 showed that drawing them in the text color leaves a solid silhouette).
- REQ-0017-07 If typing, a paste, a format change or a resize would make the box's height pass the picture's bottom edge, then the app
  shall refuse that whole edit and sound the desktop's error bell; nothing of it is done (a resize stops at the last width the text
  fits, REQ-0017-10). The first line is always allowed, also where it is taller than the picture: its part outside the picture is not
  shown and not put down (JOD, 2026-10-26, as XP). If the memory to show the box's text after an edit cannot be had, the edit is
  refused with the error bell (this requirement already uses one for an edit past the bottom edge), and nothing of it is done; a box
  whose first text cannot be shown is closed with nothing put down, and the error dialog with the heading "Could Not Add the Text" and
  the body "There is not enough memory to show this text." is shown once (JOD, 2026-10-26).
- REQ-0017-08 Undo (Ctrl+Z, the header bar) while a box is open shall take back one edit of the box. An edit is: a run of typed
  characters (the run ends when the caret is moved by a key or a press, or with any other edit); a run of Backspace, or of Delete;
  a paste; a cut; a format change of selected text; a finished move or resize. A format change with no text selected changes no text
  and is not an edit (it applies to the next typed run, and is forgotten if the caret moves first). With no edit left, Undo closes the
  box and puts nothing down: no step, the redo steps of the picture kept. Redo (Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y, the header bar) redoes the box's
  edits while it is open; a new edit drops those that could be redone (JOD, 2026-10-26). After Undo the caret and the selected text
  are as they were before the edit taken back (Undo of a Bold selects that text again); after Redo they are as they were right after
  the edit was first made (Redo of a typed character: caret after it; of a Bold: the text still selected; of a delete: caret where the
  deleted text was); a move or resize leaves the caret where it is. While a box is open Undo is sensitive, and Redo is sensitive only
  while a box edit can be redone, whatever redo steps the picture has (as REQ-0011-11). An edit takes, when it is made, the memory its
  own Undo and Redo will need, so that Undo and Redo of a box edit never fail for memory; if that memory, or the memory for the edit
  itself, cannot be had, the edit is refused as REQ-0017-07 says for the memory to show the text: the error bell and nothing of it
  done, and a box whose first text it was closes with nothing put down and the error dialog shown once.

### Moving and resizing the box
- REQ-0017-09 The box's move band is a band 4 logical pixels wide just outside the box. Outside the box a press goes, first to a box
  handle's hit area (REQ-0015-02), then to the band, then to a picture resize handle's hit area (REQ-0004-16); inside the box a press
  always goes to the text, also where a picture handle's hit area reaches in (JOD's rule of REQ-0015-02 that a selection handle owns
  that part does not apply: a box handle never lies inside the box). When the user presses the primary button on the band and drags,
  the app shall move the box with the pointer, the grab point keeping its place on the box, the box kept wholly inside the picture (a
  box as tall as the picture moves only sideways); its text moves with it unchanged.
- REQ-0017-10 When the user presses the primary button on a box handle and drags, the app shall change the box's left, top, width
  and set height as a selection handle does (REQ-0015-03: the opposite side or corner stays, the pointer's movement moves the dragged
  side), without scaling the text: the lines wrap again at the new width. The box stays inside the picture and is at least 1 pixel wide;
  its set height may go below its text's height, but the box is never shorter than its text; a width at which the text would pass the
  picture's bottom edge is not taken (the drag stops at the last width that fits). A secondary press on a box handle does nothing.
- REQ-0017-11 During a move or a handle drag, the other button, Esc or Undo shall cancel it, the box back as at the press; the other
  inputs during the drag are as during the drag of REQ-0017-04, also a release that never arrives or a lost focus. Each finished move
  or resize is one edit of the box (REQ-0017-08). The arrow keys move the caret, never the box.

### Formatting
- REQ-0017-12 Each character of a box shall have its own font family, size, color, and Bold, Italic, Underline and Strikethrough
  (new-behaviors.md). Typed text takes the style at the caret, its color included (JOD, 2026-10-26). A change of one of them (by the
  font card, a shortcut or a color pick, REQ-0017-13) applies to the selected text as one edit; with no text selected,
  to the text typed next at the caret (REQ-0017-08). While a box is open (JOD, 2026-10-27): Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I and Ctrl+U toggle Bold,
  Italic and Underline, and Ctrl+Shift+5 toggles Strikethrough (the key that writes 5, whatever the layout writes with Shift); Ctrl+]
  and Ctrl+[ make the size 1 point larger or smaller, within 1 to 500: each part of the selected text from its own size, a part at the
  limit keeping its size, or with nothing selected the text typed next. Each press is one edit (Undo takes one press back); a press
  that changes no size does nothing (no bell, no edit). Ctrl+I is then not taken by an Image action. A new box starts with the starting style: the style at the caret of the last box put
  down or closed in this run, or at the first box of a run the style of the settings (REQ-0017-24; Preferences will edit it later);
  its color is the foreground color at the moment it is made (JOD, 2026-10-26).
- REQ-0017-13 A color pick shall be a primary press on a palette swatch (REQ-0003-06) or a foreground color chosen in the color
  chooser (REQ-0003); there is no other way to color text (JOD, 2026-10-26). While a box is open it makes the foreground color that
  color, as everywhere, and also: with text selected, colors that part only, its color glyphs excepted (one edit); with nothing selected, sets the color of the
  text typed next at the caret and leaves the existing text alone (an empty box takes it before its first letter). To color all of a
  box's text the user selects all first (Ctrl+A, REQ-0017-19; new-behaviors.md). A double-click on a swatch is a press (one edit) and
  then the chooser's choice (another). Moving the caret into text of another color does not change the foreground color, and a swap of
  the colors changes no text. The background color is never per part: it fills the whole box in Opaque mode, follows a change of the
  background color while the box is open, and a Transparent box has no fill. (Pointer: REQ-0003-09 says choosing colors changes no picture; with a box open it changes the box's text, not the picture.)

### The font card
- REQ-0017-14 While a box is open, the app shall show a font card over the canvas area, not part of the picture and not moving it
  (JOD, 2026-10-26, replacing a bar under the header bar): above the box with a gap of 8 logical pixels; below it when there is no room
  above in the view; shifted sideways to stay inside the view; never over the box, except that when the box leaves no room above or
  below it in the view, the card sits at the view's top edge. It follows the box through a move, a resize, scrolling and zooming, is
  hidden while the box is wholly out of view, and cannot be dragged. It is the only font control (JOD, 2026-10-26: there is no font
  dialog). It holds one row (JOD, 2026-10-26): the font family, the size (REQ-0017-25), and Bold, Italic, Underline and Strikethrough
  toggles; it has no color button, as the colors are picked on the palette (REQ-0017-13). The family is a button with the accessible name
  "Font family" that shows the family and opens a popover (also by Ctrl+Shift+F while a box is open, with the typing in its search;
  JOD, 2026-10-27): a search field at its top, filtering the installed families as the user
  types (anywhere in the name, case ignored), over a list that can be scrolled to choose one, opened with the current family scrolled
  to and highlighted, and none highlighted when the selection's families differ (JOD, 2026-10-26). Typing starts the search; Up and
  Down move in the list, Enter picks, and Esc closes only the popover (the card and the box stay); a pick always applies, also of a
  family that part of the selection already has; after a pick or Esc the keys go to the box again. Each family's name is written in its
  own font; a font that cannot show its own name (a symbol font) is written in the interface font. A family of the box that is not
  installed shows on the button by its stored name, and the rules for it stay (REQ-0017-20, REQ-0017-24). After a change made on the card,
  and however the popover closes (a pick, Esc, its button, a press elsewhere, the window losing and getting back the focus), the keys
  go to the box and no control of the card keeps a focus ring. Each control's tooltip names it and its key: "Font family
  (Ctrl+Shift+F)", "Font size (Ctrl+] larger, Ctrl+[ smaller)", "Bold (Ctrl+B)", "Italic (Ctrl+I)", "Underline (Ctrl+U)",
  "Strikethrough (Ctrl+Shift+5)". Generic font names (Sans, Serif, Monospace and fontconfig's other
  aliases) are not listed; a generic name is shown as the installed family it stands for. The card shows the style at the caret or of the selected
  text: a family or size that differs within the selection shows as an empty field, a style only some of it has shows as off. The card's controls take no keyboard focus (JOD, 2026-10-27): they are used with the mouse, and from the keyboard through the box's
  shortcuts (Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U). Only the size field takes the typing, after a click in its text until Enter, Esc or a press
  elsewhere, and the family's search field while the popover is open; there Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V act on the field; Enter applies the field and Esc leaves it as it was,
  both giving the focus back to the box; a field that loses the focus otherwise (a press in the box or on the canvas) applies its value
  first, and then the press acts. An empty field applied changes nothing.
- REQ-0017-25 The size field of the card shall be a spin field with − and + buttons, step 1, from 1 to 500,
  taking digits only; a typed number outside 1 to 500 is clamped to it when the field is applied; there are no presets (JOD, 2026-10-26).
  A press on − or +, and Up or Down or a turn of the mouse wheel over the field while the field has the focus (after a click in its
  text, REQ-0017-14), one notch at a time, each do what Ctrl+[ or Ctrl+] does (REQ-0017-12): each part of the selected text 1 point
  smaller or larger from its own size, a part at a limit keeping its size, one edit a step, nothing at all when no size changes.
  Page Up and Page Down in the field step by 10 the same way, each part from its own size and clamped to 1 to 500: a part at 497 goes
  to 500, a part already at the limit in that direction keeps its size. Without the focus in the field, the mouse wheel over it goes to
  the canvas as anywhere over the canvas area (it scrolls the view) and changes no size: the card floats over the canvas, and a
  stray pointer must not change the text. With mixed sizes the field stays empty after a step (JOD, 2026-10-27).

### Putting the text down
- REQ-0017-16 Putting a box down shall write its text into the picture where the box is, hard-edged (P4): a pixel takes a character's
  color (a color glyph's own color at that pixel, REQ-0017-06), in all four channels, where that character's glyph, underline or strikethrough covers at least half of it, and keeps its own
  color otherwise, with no partial colors; where two characters cover the same pixel, the later one in the text wins. A foreground
  with an alpha below 255 is written as it is (as the Pencil writes it). In Opaque mode the whole box is first filled with the background
  color. Only pixels inside the picture change. The box's making, its edits and the put-down are one undo step (REQ-0002-01) and the
  title gets the modified mark; a box with no characters puts nothing down in either mode and adds no step, nor does one whose put-down
  changes no pixel (REQ-0010-07: a Transparent box of spaces). A step added discards the redo steps (REQ-0002-05). The step costs at most
  what REQ-0002-16 allows for the pixels it changes; one larger than the whole budget is kept as the only step (REQ-0002-17).
- REQ-0017-17 An open box shall be put down by: a primary or secondary press with Text outside the box, its band and its handles (it
  makes no new box); Esc (when no menu or the card's family popover is open, which Esc closes instead); another tool; Save, Save As, New,
  Open, Quit; an Image action; a primary press on a picture resize handle; Paste From…, once its file has been read (a cancelled or
  failed Paste From… leaves the box open, as REQ-0013-11's pointer note for a floating selection). Each of these then goes on, and the
  box stays put down if a prompt or file dialog of the action is then cancelled. Enter does not put it down (it starts a new line), nor
  does Select All (it selects the text), choosing Text again, view actions, zooming, scrolling, a resize of the window or a lost focus;
  the box has no time limit. A quit request puts the box down and then asks about unsaved changes (REQ-0001-35).
- REQ-0017-18 If the memory for the put-down's undo step cannot be had, then the app shall show an error dialog with the heading "Could
  Not Add the Text" and the body "There is not enough memory to add this text.", leave the picture as it was and the box open with its
  text, and not do the action that asked for the put-down (JOD, 2026-10-26; unlike a selection, REQ-0013-19, whose pixels go back to
  their place, a box has no place to go back to): a press outside makes no box; Esc leaves the box open; a tool change leaves Text the
  tool; Save, Save As, New, Open and Quit show no dialog or prompt and save or close nothing; an Image action, Paste From… and a press
  on a picture resize handle are not done (Paste From…'s read picture is dropped).

### Clipboard
- REQ-0017-19 While a box is open, Cut, Copy and Paste (Ctrl+X, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, the Edit submenu) shall act on the box's text, not on
  the picture (pointer: REQ-0016-01, -02). Cut and Copy are sensitive while text is selected in the box; they put the selected text on
  the clipboard as plain text and as rich text with each part's font family, size, color and styles (new-behaviors.md), so that
  LibreOffice Writer and a web page's rich-text field paste it with its formatting; Cut then removes it (one edit). Copy To… and Clear
  Selection are insensitive while a box is open; Select All (Ctrl+A, the Edit submenu) selects all the box's text (pointer:
  REQ-0013-15). If the rich text of the selected text cannot get its memory, "Could Not Copy the Text" shows with the body "There is
  not enough memory to copy this text.", the clipboard and the box stay as they were, and Cut removes nothing (JOD, 2026-10-27: the
  words name the text, as REQ-0016-11's name the selection).
- REQ-0017-20 Paste while a box is open shall be sensitive while the clipboard offers text (plain or rich, the paths Files offers with
  copied files included), and insert it at the caret, replacing the selected text, as one edit: rich text keeps each part's family,
  size, color and styles, except that a family not installed takes the family of the text it lands in, the family at the caret
  (its own size, color and styles kept; JOD, 2026-10-26), and what the box cannot hold (links, highlight colors, pictures, tables, alignment) is dropped, its text kept; plain text
  takes the style at the caret. A clipboard offering a picture and text pastes the text while a box is open; one offering a picture and
  no text makes Paste insensitive then. Paste as Plain Text (Ctrl+Shift+V and the box's menu; sensitive as Paste) inserts the text alone
  with the style at the caret. Line breaks of any kind become new lines; a tab becomes a space. A size in pasted rich text is taken as
  1 to 500 points, the nearest of the two when it is outside them; the HTML sizes 1 to 7 of <font size> are 8, 10, 12, 14, 18, 24 and
  36 points; a size in pixels is taken at 0.75 point a pixel (96 pixels an inch); a size is rounded to the nearest whole point before
  it is taken as 1 to 500; a size in any other unit (em, %, a keyword such as "large") is taken as not given: the part takes the size
  at the caret (JOD, 2026-10-27: what the reader does, stated). If the clipboard's rich text cannot
  be read into the box for want of memory, "Could Not Paste" shows with the body "There is not enough memory to paste this text." and
  nothing changes; an edit the box refuses for memory is REQ-0017-07's (the error bell).
- REQ-0017-27 Paste and Paste as Plain Text shall wait for the clipboard's text as REQ-0016-15 waits for a picture: input waits and the
  busy cursor shows; if nothing has arrived within 5 seconds, "Could Not Paste" shows with the body "The clipboard's text cannot be
  read." and nothing changes; text that arrives after a quit request is dropped and the quit goes on.
- REQ-0017-21 While no box is open, text on the clipboard shall not make Paste sensitive: text is pasted only into an open box
  (JOD, 2026-10-26; REQ-0016-02 stays as it is). A clipboard holding a picture and text pastes the picture (REQ-0016-05).

### The box's menu
- REQ-0017-22 When the user presses the secondary button with Text inside an open box or on its band, the app shall open a context
  menu at the pointer (a GNOME popover menu, as REQ-0016-14) with, in its first section, Cut, Copy, Paste, Paste as Plain Text
  (Ctrl+Shift+V); in its second, Select All (JOD, 2026-10-26; new-behaviors.md). Each item is sensitive as its
  counterpart is (REQ-0017-19, -20) and acts as it does. A secondary press inside the box on text that is not selected first places the
  caret there; on selected text it keeps the selection. With Text active and no box open, a secondary press in the canvas area does
  nothing.
- REQ-0017-23 While Text is active and no box is open, the Edit submenu and the shortcuts of REQ-0016-01 shall be as 0016 has them with
  no selection (Cut, Copy and Copy To… insensitive); with a box open they follow REQ-0017-19 to REQ-0017-21.

### Settings
- REQ-0017-24 The starting style of a run shall come from the settings file (REQ-0001-01, REQ-0001-40), section `[text]`: `font`, a
  family name as the font list shows it (matched without regard to case, spaces round it trimmed) or `auto`, the desktop's interface font
  (GNOME's "Interface Text", Ubuntu Sans on this desktop; JOD, 2026-10-27); a generic name, in `font` or from the desktop, becomes the
  installed family it stands for (Sans: Noto Sans here); a name that does not resolve stays as written; `size`, a whole number from 1 to 500; `bold`, `italic`, `underline`, `strikethrough`, each `true` or `false` (any
  case). A missing, empty or bad value, or a family that is not installed, takes the default: `auto`, 12, and `false` for the styles.
  (Pointer: this adds booleans and a case-insensitive name to the syntax of REQ-0001-40.) The app reads these at start-up and never
  writes them back: what the user picks lasts for the run (as `jpeg-quality`, REQ-0005-14). A settings file the app creates gets the
  section with the defaults (pointer: REQ-0001-04); an existing one is not changed.

## Scenarios

outside the box at 100 % unless a zoom is named. Unless said otherwise the picture is 300 × 200 white (#FFFFFFFF), the foreground
black, the background yellow (#FFFF00FF), the starting style DejaVu Sans 12 regular, Transparent.

## Edge cases
- A picture shorter than one line: the box is as tall as the picture; the first line is allowed and only its part inside the picture
  is shown and put down (REQ-0017-03, -07).
- A box under about 13 logical pixels wide or tall (at 100 %, a one-line box of a small font): the handles' hit areas cover the band on
  that side, so the box is moved by another side's band or after zooming in (REQ-0017-09).
- A double-click outside an open box: the first press puts the box down; the second press, with no box open, makes a click box
  (REQ-0017-03, -17).
- A paste of very long text: refused as a whole if it passes the bottom edge (REQ-0017-07); a size of 500 on a small picture: the first
  line is allowed and cut at the edge.
- A foreground with alpha 0: the text writes #00000000 pixels, holes, as the Pencil does with that color (REQ-0017-16).
- Undo with the color chooser open: not taken (REQ-0002-11, REQ-0003-14).
- Text in the window's title, the clipboard of another app or a file name is not affected by the box's style.

## Out of scope
- Smooth (antialiased) text: Phase 2 (new-behaviors.md).
- Vertical text (XP's option for East Asian fonts), per-paragraph alignment, links, highlight colors.
- Editing text after it is put down (it is pixels then, as XP).
- Text pasted outside a box (REQ-0017-21).
- Remembering the starting style between runs (REQ-0017-24: the settings give it; Preferences may change the default).
- Keyboard ways to make, move or resize a box: Accessibility.

## Plan

Notes from the spec step (JOD, 2026-10-26): the font card is a child widget placed over the canvas area (an overlay child), not a
GtkPopover, because the isolated test session cannot test a popover's keyboard focus; the family popover's list is tested through
the card's own hooks for the same reason. There is no font dialog (GTK's font chooser was not wanted: no underline, strikethrough or
color). The card is drawn as a libadwaita card (style class `card`), one row. From the INDEX row: the rich format is HTML with inline
styles plus plain UTF-8 text, fixed only after the probe of R6; Ctrl+V with no box open does not paste text (REQ-0017-21); text and a
picture together paste the picture with no box open and the text with a box open (REQ-0017-20); a missing family takes the family at
the caret (REQ-0017-20); the clipboard seam of 0016 offers and reads two formats of one content at once.

### Approach
- The text is Easel's own, not a GTK text widget's: a GTK widget would draw antialiased text and lay it out at screen size, while
  REQ-0017-05 wants the box to show exactly the pixels the put-down writes, at every zoom. So the box's text lives in a pure-C buffer
  (characters and style runs, with the undo list of REQ-0017-08), is laid out by Pango at picture scale (1 pixel = 1 picture pixel,
  96 dpi, hinting and hint metrics off, so wrapping does not depend on the screen or the zoom), and is rasterized once per change into
  an "ink" image and a mask: a pixel is written where a character covers at least half of it (REQ-0017-16). The canvas shows the box
  as it shows a floating selection: the picture's pixels under the box with the Opaque fill and the ink applied, as one texture drawn
  with the nearest filter; the outline, handles, caret and the selected text's accent are drawn over it at screen resolution.
- Rasterizing: each style run is drawn in turn, in the order of the text, into an A8 surface (gray antialiasing, as coverage) with the
  layout's other runs at foreground alpha 1/65535, and the pixels it covers at least half are written into the ink with the run's
  color, so the later character wins where two cover a pixel; underline and strikethrough come with the run (Pango draws them in the
  foreground color). Risk R1 and R2 probe this before anything relies on it.
- Editing: a GtkIMContext on the canvas gives input methods, compose and dead keys (REQ-0017-06); the caret, selection, word moves and
  hit testing use the same PangoLayout (pango_layout_xy_to_index, _move_cursor_visually, the log attributes for words), so a press
  places the caret on the pixels the user sees.
- Putting down: a pure-C function writes the ink, the mask and the Opaque fill into the picture through the change log, as one step
  (REQ-0017-16). Every put-down goes through `easel_canvas_put_down` (and `_keeping_marked` for Save), which already put down a
  floating selection and a pending curve; it now puts the box down first, returns FALSE when the step's memory cannot be had, and every
  caller stops on FALSE (REQ-0017-18; the callers are listed below).
- The font card is a child of a GtkOverlay that wraps the canvas's scrolled window (the notes above: not a GtkPopover), placed from the
  box's rectangle in view coordinates on every allocation, scroll, zoom and box change.
- The clipboard: a provider of two MIME types, `text/html` (an HTML fragment with inline styles) and `text/plain;charset=utf-8`, through
  the seam of 0016; Paste reads `text/html` first. The HTML reader and writer are pure C (a subset: span styles, b, i, u, s, strike,
  font, br, p, div; entities); what the box cannot hold is dropped (REQ-0017-20).

### What grows
- The buffer: the UTF-8 text and one run (about 32 bytes: the interned family pointer, size, flags, color, start) per change of style;
  family names interned once per family. The undo list: per edit the inserted or removed text and the runs it covered, and for a
  geometry edit the old and new rectangle; it lives only while the box is open and is not under the undo budget (the step at the
  put-down is, REQ-0017-16).
- The ink and mask: 5 bytes per pixel of the text's area (not the whole box: Opaque is a flag), made again at each change; a box as
  wide as an 8000-pixel picture with 40 lines of 16 pixels is 8000 × 760 × 5 = about 30 MB. Its allocation failing refuses the edit
  (REQ-0017-07, AS-24).
- The put-down step: 12 bytes per changed pixel through the change log, shrunk to its length before it is pushed (held against counted
  test in core).
