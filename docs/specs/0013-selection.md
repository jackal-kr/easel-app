# 0013 Selection

Status: implemented
Depends on: 0001, 0002, 0003, 0004, 0005, 0006, 0007, 0010, 0011, 0012

## Why
XP Paint's Select tool marks a rectangle of the picture so that it can be moved, copied, cleared or (later) cut, pasted and
transformed. It is the base of the Clipboard and Image Transforms features. This feature brings the rectangle Select tool, the
floating selection that moves and is put down as one undo step, Ctrl to move a copy, the arrow keys, Clear Selection,
Select All, the Opaque / Transparent option and the selection's context menu.
Decisions by JOD on 2026-10-20: Selection is split in two (Free-Form Select, Shift smear and stamp and stretching by handles
are the feature Free-form Selection); Esc and Enter put a floating selection down, Undo cancels it; the arrow keys move it;
a partial context menu here; Select All and Clear Selection in an Edit submenu of the primary menu; still dashes for the
outline; the hole left by a lift takes the background color; three mock-ups (described in REQ-0013-02, -16 and -17).

## Requirements
Throughout, a selection is a rectangle of picture pixels. It is **marked** while its pixels are still part of the picture,
and **floating** after it has been lifted (moved, by the pointer or the keys): then its pixels are held apart from the
picture, shown where they will be put down, and the area they came from shows the hole of REQ-0013-08. A floating selection
is pending (`docs/design.md`, Pending state) until it is put down (REQ-0013-11). A press is **on the selection** when it is on
one of its pixels (marked or floating, inside or outside the picture); the outline and the space beside it are not.

### The tool
- REQ-0013-01 The toolbox shall show Select, with the tooltip "Select", after the Magnifier in the final order of REQ-0006-01, so that the toolbox is, in two columns, row by row: Pan, Magnifier / Select, Eraser / Pick Color, Fill / Gradient, Pencil / Brush, Airbrush / Line, Curve / Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle / Ellipse, Polygon. Clicking it makes it the active tool (REQ-0001-09); clicking it while it is active keeps the selection as it is (as REQ-0011-12). Numpad + and Numpad − do nothing with Select (as REQ-0007-14); Ctrl+Numpad + and − keep zooming (REQ-0004-04).
- REQ-0013-02 While Select is active, the options panel (REQ-0006-02) shall show two mode buttons drawn exactly as the Eraser's mode presets (REQ-0006-10), one above the other: Opaque first (a filled square) and Transparent (a dashed square outline), the chosen one highlighted (JOD, 2026-10-20, at M1: like the Eraser's, not the mock-up's dotted rectangles), with the tooltips "Opaque: keep background color" and "Transparent: moving or clearing leaves a transparent hole". Opaque is chosen at each start; the mode is kept while the app runs, is the selection's own (the Eraser's mode is separate), and is not a setting (as the Eraser's mode). Changing it while a selection floats changes nothing of it; it counts at the next lift and the next Clear Selection (REQ-0013-08, -13, -14).
- REQ-0013-03 The status bar hint (REQ-0004-09) shall be "Selects a rectangular part of the picture to move, copy, or edit." While Select is active the cursor over the canvas area shall be the crosshair of REQ-0010-15, and over the selection the move cursor; the resize cursor wins over a handle (REQ-0004-17). (Pointer note, Selection handles, 2026-10-22: over a handle of the selection the cursor is its resize cursor.)

### Marking a selection
- REQ-0013-04 When the user presses the primary button with Select in the canvas area, not on the selection and not on a resize handle, the app shall first put down a floating selection and remove any selection (REQ-0013-11), then show, while the button is held, the rectangle from the pixel under the press to the pixel under the pointer (both included, whichever corner the pointer is at), limited to the picture, without changing the picture. A press may start outside the picture but inside the canvas area, and while the button is held the pointer may go outside the picture, the canvas area or the window (REQ-0010-21). Shift makes it a square, of side the smaller of its width and height, from the press toward the pointer (as REQ-0010-14), the square being made first and then limited to the picture; pressing or releasing Shift while the button is held changes it at once. The release leaves the rectangle marked as the selection. A release on the press pixel (a click), or a rectangle with no pixel inside the picture, leaves no selection. If putting a floating selection down by this press fails (REQ-0013-19), the press starts no rectangle. (Pointer note, Selection handles, 2026-10-22: "resize handle" here means the picture's; a primary press on a selection handle stretches the selection and neither puts it down nor removes it.)
- REQ-0013-05 The selection shall be outlined, marked or floating, by a line 1 logical pixel wide just outside its pixels, in dashes of 4 logical pixels alternating black and white (as the gradient's guide line, REQ-0012-13), not animated, over the picture and the canvas area and not part of the picture. It is shown while the rectangle is dragged out and while a selection exists, at every zoom, and scrolls with the picture. (Pointer note, 0014 REQ-0014-17, 2026-10-21, replacing "1 logical pixel wide" and "not animated": the thickness grows with the zoom, the dashes are longer with it and move, the four sides are one line counted clockwise from the top-left corner, and the selected pixels are tinted.) (Pointer note, Selection handles, 2026-10-22: eight handles are drawn round the selection's bounding rectangle.)
- REQ-0013-06 The status bar shall show the selection's size as "W × H" (its width and height in picture pixels, without a unit) just before the picture size (REQ-0004-09, REQ-0004-11): while a rectangle is dragged out, the size of its part inside the picture (nothing while it has none); while a selection exists, its whole size (also when it floats partly outside the picture); nothing otherwise.

### Moving a selection
- REQ-0013-07 When the user presses the primary button on the selection and drags, the app shall move the selection with the pointer: it keeps its place relative to the pointer, moving by the pointer's movement in whole picture pixels (the pixel under the pointer minus the pixel under the press). A marked selection is lifted at the first pointer movement to another pixel, not at the press; a press and release without such a movement changes nothing. A floating selection may be moved partly or wholly outside the picture and back; while it floats it keeps all its pixels, drawn over the canvas area outside the picture too, and a press on its pixels there grabs it; the scrollable area does not grow for it. The release leaves it floating. Ctrl+Shift acts as Ctrl. (Pointer note, 0014, replacing "Shift at the press or during the move changes nothing": Shift at the press of a move now smears, and a Shift-click on a floating selection stamps, with Select too: REQ-0014-11, REQ-0014-12; Shift pressed or released after the press still changes nothing.)
- REQ-0013-08 When a marked selection is lifted, the area it occupied (the hole) shall become fully transparent (#00000000) if the mode is Transparent at the lift, and take the background color current at the lift, exactly in all four channels, if it is Opaque (JOD, 2026-10-20, at M2); the hole is fixed at the lift, a later change of the mode or of the background color leaves it as it is; the floating pixels are shown over the picture as they will be put down.
- REQ-0013-09 While Ctrl is held at the press of a move drag, the app shall move a copy: a marked selection is lifted without leaving a hole (the picture keeps those pixels); a floating selection leaves a copy of its pixels in the picture where it is (as they would be put down there, REQ-0013-11) and moves on. The copies left belong to the one step of REQ-0013-11.
- REQ-0013-10 While a selection exists and no dialog, prompt, menu or the color chooser is open, the arrow keys shall move it by 1 picture pixel (left, right, up, down) and Shift with an arrow by 10, wherever the keyboard focus is in the window; each key repeat moves again; the first such key lifts a marked selection as a move does (REQ-0013-07, REQ-0013-08), and all the moves join the one step of REQ-0013-11. Arrows with Ctrl or Alt are not taken. While a mouse button is held the arrow keys do nothing to the selection. Without a selection the arrow keys are not taken (focus moves as usual).

### Putting it down, removing it
- REQ-0013-11 Putting a floating selection down shall write its pixels into the picture where it is, each replacing the picture's pixel in all four channels (no blending; only those inside the picture), and remove the selection. The lift, the copies of REQ-0013-09, every move and the put-down together are one undo step (REQ-0002-01) and the title gets the modified mark; if no pixel of the picture differs from before the lift, no step is added and no mark (REQ-0010-07). A step added discards the redo steps (REQ-0002-05); a put-down that adds none keeps them. The step costs at most what REQ-0002-16 allows for the pixels it changes; a step larger than the whole budget is kept as the only step (REQ-0002-17). A floating selection is put down by: a press outside it with Select (which then marks a new one, REQ-0013-04), Esc, Enter (the main keyboard's and the keypad's), another tool, Save, Save As, New, Open, Quit, an Image action, a primary press on a resize handle, Select All (REQ-0013-15) and a change of the picture's size; each of these then goes on, and the selection stays put down if a prompt or file dialog of the action is then cancelled. (Pointer note, Selection handles, 2026-10-22: "resize handle" here means the picture's; a primary press on a selection handle stretches the selection and neither puts it down nor removes it.) (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: Paste and Paste From… also put a floating selection down, but only after the picture is read and the oversize prompt answered; a cancelled prompt or Paste From dialog leaves it floating, REQ-0016-05, -07, -08.)
- REQ-0013-12 Each event of the list of REQ-0013-11 shall also remove a marked selection, except Save and Save As, after which it stays marked; removing a marked selection changes nothing in the picture. Undo while a selection floats shall cancel the lift: every pixel is as before the lift (the copies of REQ-0013-09 gone too), the selection is removed, no step is added and the redo steps stay; while a selection floats Undo is available even if there is nothing to undo, and Redo is not (as REQ-0011-11). Undo or Redo while a selection is marked undoes or redoes as usual (REQ-0002-02, REQ-0002-03) and removes the selection. Esc, Enter and Delete (REQ-0013-14) are taken wherever the focus is in the window while a selection exists, and are not taken by the selection without one. (Pointer note, Selection handles, 2026-10-22: "resize handle" here means the picture's; a primary press on a selection handle stretches the selection and neither puts it down nor removes it.) (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: Paste removes a marked selection; Undo while a paste floats removes it, and with Resize Canvas to Picture the growth too, REQ-0016-05, -07.)

### Opaque and Transparent
- REQ-0013-13 The pixels of a floating selection shall keep their own colors in both modes, background-colored ones too (JOD, 2026-10-20, at M2): they are shown and put down as they are, replacing what is under them. The mode decides only what a lift (REQ-0013-08) and Clear Selection (REQ-0013-14) leave behind; a change of the mode or of the background color while a selection floats changes nothing of what is shown or put down. A marked selection looks like the picture in either mode.

### Clear Selection, Select All, menus
- REQ-0013-14 Clear Selection (the Delete key, the Edit submenu and the context menu) shall, while a selection exists, remove it as one undo step (no step if no pixel changes): on a marked selection, its area becomes fully transparent in Transparent mode and takes the background color current now in Opaque mode; on a floating selection, its pixels are discarded and what was done since the lift stays (the hole, if it was lifted without Ctrl, and the copies of REQ-0013-09). Without a selection it is insensitive and Delete is not taken. (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: a pasted selection has no lift: Clear Selection or Cut on it discards it and keeps the copies it left, REQ-0016-05; Cut copies first, REQ-0016-04.)
- REQ-0013-15 Select All (Ctrl+A, wherever the focus is in the window; the Edit submenu; the context menu) shall put down a floating selection (and apply or drop what is pending as another tool does, REQ-0011-12, REQ-0012-07), make Select the active tool and mark the whole picture as the selection. It is ignored when a tool change is (REQ-0001-09: during a stroke or drag; REQ-0004-03: with a dialog, the quit prompt or the color chooser open) and before the picture exists.
- REQ-0013-16 The primary menu (REQ-0005-01) shall get, between its file items and Quit and separated from both by a line, a submenu Edit holding Select All (Ctrl+A) and Clear Selection (Delete), as in JOD's mock-up of 2026-10-20 (the primary menu there: the file items and Print…; a line; Edit ›, Copy To…, Paste From…; a line; Preferences, Keyboard Shortcuts, Help, About); later features add their items to it and around it. (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: the Edit submenu becomes Cut, Copy, Paste; a line; Select All, Clear Selection, and Copy To… and Paste From… follow it in the primary menu, REQ-0016-01.)
- REQ-0013-17 When the user presses the secondary button on the selection with Select, the app shall open a context menu at the pointer holding Clear Selection and Select All (Ctrl+A); it changes nothing else. Its look and places follow JOD's mock-up of 2026-10-20: a GNOME popover menu in three sections, Cut (Ctrl+X), Copy (Ctrl+C), Paste (Ctrl+V); Clear Selection, Select All (Ctrl+A), Copy To…; Flip Horizontal, Flip Vertical, Rotate ›, Invert Colors (Ctrl+I); this feature shows only its two items in the second section and later features add theirs. A secondary press elsewhere with Select does nothing (the canvas menu comes with Clipboard), and the middle button does nothing. While a menu or popover is open, the arrow keys, Esc, Enter and Delete act on it (closing the context menu with Esc puts nothing down). (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: Cut, Copy, Paste and Copy To… join this menu, REQ-0016-01; a secondary press elsewhere with Select now opens the canvas menu, REQ-0016-14.)

### Drags that do not finish, memory, blocked input
- REQ-0013-18 During a drag of Select (a rectangle dragged out, or a move), the app shall cancel that drag when the other button is pressed, Esc is pressed or Undo is chosen (after Undo, REQ-0002-09 applies until the release): a rectangle being dragged out leaves no selection (a put-down made by its press stays); a move puts the selection back where it was before this drag (if this drag lifted it, the lift is undone and the picture is as before; if Ctrl left a copy at this drag's press, that copy goes too), and it stays selected. Any release after that does nothing. Enter, Delete, Select All, Redo and the arrow keys do nothing during the drag. If the release never arrives (REQ-0007-18) or the window loses focus (REQ-0010-19), the drag ends as last shown, as if released there; when a press shows that the release was lost, that press then acts as a press of its own. Other tools, View and Image actions are ignored during the drag as during a shape drag (REQ-0010-08); Save, Save As, New, Open and Quit end it as last shown, put a floating selection down and go on (REQ-0010-18). A color change acts at once (REQ-0013-13). (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: Cut, Copy, Paste, Copy To… and Paste From… are ignored during the drag too, REQ-0016-02.)
- REQ-0013-19 If the memory to lift a selection (by a drag, Ctrl, or an arrow key) cannot be had, then the app shall show an error dialog with the heading "Could Not Move the Selection" and the body "There is not enough memory to move this selection.", change nothing and leave the selection marked where it was; a drag in which the lift fails ends there (its motion and release do nothing). If a Ctrl drag of a floating selection cannot get the memory for the copy it leaves, the same dialog shows and the selection stays floating where it was before that drag. If the memory for the undo step cannot be had when a selection is put down, the picture shall be as before the lift (nothing moved, no copies), the selection removed, and the same dialog shown; the action that put it down goes on first and the dialog shows after it, except Quit, which stops and shows the dialog. If Clear Selection cannot get the memory for its step, the dialog is "Could Not Clear the Selection" with the body "There is not enough memory to clear this selection.", nothing changes, and a floating selection stays floating.
- REQ-0013-20 A press with Select shall be ignored when a press of a shape tool is (REQ-0010-17: before the picture exists, with a dialog, the quit prompt or the color chooser open, or during a resize drag); with a dialog, a prompt or the color chooser open, Esc, Enter, Delete and the arrow keys act on that, and a floating selection stays floating.
- REQ-0013-21 View actions, zooming, scrolling and a lost focus shall leave a marked or floating selection as it is; a floating selection has no time limit and no timer (as REQ-0011-09). Other tools do not draw inside or around the selection: choosing one puts down or removes the selection (REQ-0013-11, REQ-0013-12), and the tool then works on the whole picture.

## Edge cases
- A drag cannot make a one-pixel selection inside the picture (a return to the press pixel is a click); clipping can (from (-3, -3) to (0, 0)), and so can Select All on a 1 × 1 picture; Clear Selection on that pixel makes it the background color.
- A selection floating wholly outside the picture and put down: nothing is written, the hole remains (one step; no step after a Ctrl lift).
- Moving a floating selection back exactly onto its hole: it covers the hole entirely, so no pixel differs and no step is added, in both modes.
- The background color or the mode changed between marking and lifting: the hole takes what is current at the lift (transparent in Transparent mode, the background color in Opaque mode).
- The colors swapped (Ctrl+Shift+X) or the mode switched while a selection floats: nothing of it changes (REQ-0013-13).
- A press on the selection with Ctrl and no movement: nothing is lifted or copied.
- A selection smaller than a device pixel at a low zoom: it is grabbed only on its pixels; the arrow keys still move it.
- The largest picture, 10000 × 10000, all selected and moved by one pixel: a step for about 100 million pixels, kept as the only step or refused with the dialog (REQ-0013-19).
- The same action again while its dialog is open: as 0011.

## Plan
### Approach
The picture is not touched while a selection floats. The core keeps a **float**: the lifted pixels (w × h, straight
0xAARRGGBB), its place, the hole (its rectangle and the background color at the lift, none after a Ctrl lift) and the
stamps that Ctrl drags leave (a place). One pure function lays the result over any
rectangle of picture pixels in order: the picture, the hole, each stamp, then the float at its place. The canvas preview draws the tiles in view through it (as the gradient's preview, REQ-0012-07, the same texture
path, size, format and filter as the picture's tiles), and the put-down writes the union of the hole, the stamps and the float
through the same function, row by row, into the shared change log (`change-log`, 0012), which logs only pixels whose value
changes and is shrunk before it becomes the one step. So the preview is the put-down, a selection moved back is no step,
Undo while floating is "forget the float" (nothing to restore), and Clear on a floating selection is the put-down without the
float. A marked selection is just a rectangle; Clear on it fills it through the change log. Floating pixels outside the
picture are drawn over the canvas area as textures of the parts in view (only for the eye: AS-15 compares the picture's part).

### Where it is put down, removed or cancelled
- `finish_selection (self, keep_marked)` in the canvas is the one function that puts a float down (and removes a marked

- A drag's own cancel (the other button, Esc, Undo during a drag) goes through `cancel_selection_drag`, which puts the float
  back where this drag found it, or forgets a float this drag lifted.

### What grows
Per float: its pixels, 4 bytes each (a whole 10000 × 10000 picture lifted: 400 MB, refused with the dialog if not had), and
8 bytes per stamp (a stamps array that doubles, freed with the float). Per put-down: the log, 12 bytes per changed pixel
plus 64, shrunk to its length before the push (a test compares the heap held with `easel_history_bytes`, as 0012 T005). Per
frame: the tiles in view laid over (as 0012), and the outside part's textures (only the part in view).

### Measurements
`perf-0013`: a selection of 400 × 300 dragged at 1920 × 1080 and at 8000 × 8000, at 100 % and at the fit zoom (each move to the
painted frame, and the peak memory), and the put-down of the whole 8000 × 8000 picture moved by one pixel (time, peak memory).
The fit zoom of a large picture is expected to cost as 0012's (accepted there); anything else over budget goes to JOD.

Measured 2026-10-20 (T019), `perf-0013` in the real session, no other Easel window, three runs; each move to the painted
frame, median and worst of 30 moves, ms; peak memory before and during the drag:

| selection of 400 x 300 dragged | run 1 | run 2 | run 3 | peak memory |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1920 x 1080, 100 % | 16.6, 26.9 | 16.7, 27.1 | 16.8, 26.8 | 119 -> 131 MB |
| 1920 x 1080, fit (69 %) | 16.7, 22.5 | 16.8, 24.2 | 16.4, 23.1 | 130 -> 135 MB |
