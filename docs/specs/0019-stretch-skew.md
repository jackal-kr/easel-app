# 0019 Stretch and Skew

Status: implemented
Reviewed: spec-reviewer, 2026-11-02 (12 findings merged; JOD accepted, the merge shown old/new)
Depends on: 0002 (undo), 0004 (Image menu, resize, Attributes' number fields), 0013 (selection, its menu), 0014 (free-form selection and its line), 0015 (the scale by the nearest pixel), 0018 (what a command acts on, its keys, its memory rule), 0025 (the pixel contract)

## Why
XP Paint resizes a picture by scaling it and slants it, from one dialog; people shrink a screenshot, double a sprite or lean a drawing this way. Attributes only crops or extends, and a handle stretches only a selection and only by the eye. Easel follows XP's dialog and adds exact pixels and Keep aspect ratio (`docs/reference/new-behaviors.md`).

## Requirements
### The dialog
- REQ-0019-01 (Pointer note, Crop to selection, 2026-11-11: "Crop to Selection" follows directly after Stretch and Skew… in both menus, REQ-0041-01.) The Image menu's section of Flip and Rotate (REQ-0018-01) and the selection menu's third section (REQ-0018-02) shall hold "Stretch and Skew…", directly after Rotate › (Ctrl+T; JOD, 2026-11-02: XP's Ctrl+W closes the window here, REQ-0001-34). The item and the key follow REQ-0018-01: they act wherever the focus is in the window, on any keyboard layout, and do nothing before the picture exists, while a dialog, the quit prompt or the color chooser is open, while any mouse button is held in the canvas area, or while the focus is in a text field of the font card.
- REQ-0019-02 The item shall open a modal dialog "Stretch and Skew" (its fields as in Attributes, REQ-0004-21) with, from top to bottom: a section Stretch with a Percent | Pixels choice, Width, Height and a check box Keep aspect ratio; a section Skew with a Degrees | Pixels choice, Horizontal and Vertical; a line "New size: W × H" (the numbers it would give); and Cancel and OK. It opens with Percent, 100 and 100, Keep aspect ratio on, Degrees, and 0 and 0, every time: nothing is remembered. A typed value takes effect when the focus leaves the field or at Enter; until then the other fields and the New size line do not follow it. Enter first applies a typed value, then acts as OK only if OK is then sensitive; otherwise the dialog stays open. Esc is Cancel, which changes nothing further (REQ-0019-06). OK closes the dialog first; an error dialog then shows over the window, and the values are not kept.
- REQ-0019-03 Each number field takes whole numbers and has a minus and a plus button, and shows its value with the unit chosen (JOD, 2026-11-03): "100 %", "800 px", "45°". A typed value may have its unit after it, with or without a space ("250 %", "250%"), or none; another unit is text that is not a whole number. Its value changes by 1 with the buttons, the Up and Down keys and the wheel, by 10 with Page Up and Page Down, and by typing; a change past its limit stops at the limit, a typed number past its limit becomes the limit, and text that is not a whole number goes back to the value the field had (REQ-0004-21). The limits: Width and Height in percent 1 to 500 (XP's), in pixels 1 to 10000; Horizontal and Vertical in degrees −89 to 89 (XP's), in pixels −9999 to 9999.
- REQ-0019-04 A percent is of what the command acts on (REQ-0019-06), its size rounded to the nearest whole pixel and at least 1; every rounding in the dialog is to the nearest, halves up (as REQ-0015-07): 33 % of 400 is 132, 1 % of 50 is 1, 50 % of 3 is 2. Changing the choice converts the fields to the other unit, within the new unit's limits: 200 % of 400 shows 800 pixels; 132 pixels of 400 shows 33 %. While Keep aspect ratio is on, a change of Stretch's unit converts Width and sets Height from it by REQ-0019-05. Skew's degrees to pixels is REQ-0019-10's S; its pixels to degrees is the arctangent of S ÷ (H − 1), rounded; each with H, or for Vertical W, as at that step (Vertical uses the width after the horizontal growth). On one row or one column the field becomes 0. A conversion may change the result, and New size shows it: 9 pixels on 2 rows shows 84°, which converts back to 10 pixels; 1 pixel on 1000 rows shows 0°.
- REQ-0019-05 While Keep aspect ratio is on, a change of Width or Height sets the other to the same percent, or in pixels to the size that keeps the ratio, rounded to the nearest whole pixel and at least 1: width 800 of 400 × 300 gives height 600. A change that would take the other field past its limit stops at the largest value that keeps it within: 1 × 10000 cannot be made wider. Turning the box on sets Height from Width at once; if that would pass 10000, Width takes the largest value that keeps Height within (Width 10000 on 300 × 400 becomes 7500, Height 10000). Turning it off changes nothing. It has no effect on Skew.

### What is changed
- REQ-0019-06 Opening the dialog shall do what opening Attributes does to what waits: a pending curve, polygon or gradient is put down as shown (REQ-0011-12, REQ-0012-07) and an open text box is put down (REQ-0017-17), and they stay put down if the dialog is cancelled; a text box whose put-down cannot get its memory stays open and the dialog does not open (REQ-0017-18). A selection is left as it is: OK acts as REQ-0018-03 says for a Flip, on the selection while one exists (marked or floating, with Select or Free-Form Select), lifting a marked one (REQ-0018-07), and on the whole picture otherwise; Cancel leaves it as it was. The dialog's percents and New size are of the selection's size if one exists, and of the picture's otherwise.
- REQ-0019-07 The new size shall be the stretch's width and height, plus the skew's growth (REQ-0019-10). OK is insensitive, and the line reads "Too large: W × H; the most is 10000 × 10000", while either number of a new picture's size would pass 10000. A selection's new size has the same limit (REQ-0015-03). Nothing else makes OK insensitive.
- REQ-0019-08 A stretch comes first, then the horizontal skew, then the vertical skew.
- REQ-0019-09 A stretch shall scale by the nearest pixel, as REQ-0015-05 says: 4 pixels a b c d to 2 give b d, to 8 give a a b b c c d d; 1 pixel to 3 gives three of it; every pixel keeps all four channels exactly.
- REQ-0019-10 A horizontal skew shall shift each row by a whole number of pixels. Its amount S is, in pixels, the field's value, and in degrees (H − 1) × the tangent of the angle, rounded to the nearest whole number, on the height H at that step. A positive value moves the rows of larger y toward larger x: a row y goes to larger x by y × S ÷ (H − 1), rounded (S = 2 on 5 rows gives 0, 1, 1, 2, 2). For a negative value a row's shift is |S| − round (y × |S| ÷ (H − 1)), so the rows of smaller y lie at larger x (S = −2 on 5 rows gives 2, 1, 1, 0, 0; S = −3 gives 3, 2, 1, 1, 0). The width grows by |S|. What the command acts on, if it has one row at that step, does not shift and its width stays. A vertical skew is the same with x and y swapped: positive moves the columns of larger x toward larger y. Examples: 45° on 100 × 100 gives 199 × 100; 10° on 400 × 300 gives 453 × 300; 45° on one row changes nothing; 45° Horizontal and 45° Vertical on 100 × 100 gives 199 × 298 (the vertical skew's S is 198).
- REQ-0019-11 On the whole picture, the pixels that no row or column reaches shall take the background color current at OK, exactly, in all four channels (a transparent background gives #00000000), in the Opaque and the Transparent mode alike, as REQ-0004-20 fills a grown picture. Stretch and Skew change the picture's size as a resize does: the zoom stays, the scroll is kept as far as the new size allows (REQ-0004-25) and the status bar shows the new size (REQ-0004-09).
- REQ-0019-12 On a selection: a stretch does what a stretch by a handle does (REQ-0015-05, REQ-0018-07: it starts from the pixels as lifted, through the flips and turns made, so a stretch back gives them exactly). A skew makes the shown pixels the new lifted pixels, so a later stretch starts from the skewed ones. The pixels that no row or column reaches are not part of the selection (JOD, 2026-11-02): what lies under them stays, in Opaque and Transparent mode alike. A skewed rectangle therefore has a shape as a free-form selection has, and a free-form selection's shape is skewed with its pixels. After a skew the line (REQ-0014-05) is the outline of the new shape along the grid lines, one closed line for each part, parts that touch only at a corner counting as two (3 × 2 with S = 5 has two parts: the rows lie at x 0 to 2 and 5 to 7). That outline is the traced path for later stretches and turns (REQ-0015-06, REQ-0018-07), and a press on the exposed pixels is a press outside the selection (REQ-0014-04). A skewed paste has the shape too, and its fully transparent pixels stay not put down (REQ-0016-06). After a skew the sum of flips and turns (REQ-0018-07) starts again from none. The selection's smallest x and smallest y stay; the box takes the new size, the status bar shows it (REQ-0013-06), its handles follow (REQ-0015-01), it floats afterwards, and it may reach outside the picture (REQ-0013-07). Copies left before keep their pixels.
- REQ-0019-13 If every S of REQ-0019-10 is 0 or does not apply (one row, one column) and the size is unchanged, OK shall change nothing and add no step, and a marked selection stays marked (1° on 2 rows, 5 pixels on one row).

### Undo, memory and speed
- REQ-0019-14 On the whole picture OK shall be one undo step (REQ-0002-01) that gives the size and every pixel back exactly on Undo and the same result on Redo, the fill color included, costing what a quarter turn costs: 4 bytes for every pixel of the picture before it, plus 64 bytes (REQ-0018-09). On a selection it joins the one step of its lift, moves, stretches and put-down (REQ-0018-08). Undo and Redo of it cannot fail, as REQ-0018-10 says for a turn.
- REQ-0019-15 If an allocation OK needs (the new pixels or the undo step) cannot be had, the app shall show an error dialog, heading "Could Not Stretch or Skew the Picture" or "Could Not Stretch or Skew the Selection" and body "There is not enough memory for this change." (REQ-0018-10), and change nothing: the picture, the history and the selection as before OK, marked again if OK lifted it. If the lift itself cannot get its memory, REQ-0013-19's dialog shows and the selection stays marked. A pending shape or box put down before that stays put down.
- REQ-0019-16 Stretching or skewing an 8000 × 8000 picture, or a floating selection of that size, shall take less than 500 ms in the core when the new size has at most 64 million pixels (as REQ-0018-12), and less than 1 s at the largest allowed, 10000 × 10000; the result is painted at the next refresh after it.

### How it meets P7 (the pixel contract)
- REQ-0019-17 Point 1, exact colors: every new pixel is a copy of one old pixel in all four channels, or the exact background color; there is no smoothing and no new color. Point 2, the view only magnifies: nothing here changes how the picture is shown, and the dialog has no preview over the view. Point 3, preview and put-down: a selection after OK floats, and its shown pixels are exactly what its put-down writes, as REQ-0025-03 says. Point 4, same input, same pixels: the result depends only on the size, the pixels and the numbers in the fields, never on the zoom, the scroll or the screen scale; a stretch and a skew in pixels use whole numbers only, and degrees become a whole S by REQ-0019-10's rounding before any pixel moves. Points 5 and 6 are not touched.

## Edge cases
- A 1 × 1 picture with no stretch: any skew changes nothing (REQ-0019-10), since it has one row and one column. With a stretch the skew applies to the stretched size: 300 % both ways with 45° Horizontal gives 5 × 3.
- Horizontal and Vertical both set: the vertical skew uses the width the horizontal one made.
- Skew in degrees on a large picture: 89° gives 5672 on 100 rows, and on 8000 rows far past 10000: OK goes insensitive (REQ-0019-07).
- A selection partly outside the picture: its pixels outside are stretched and skewed with it; only what lies over the picture is written at its put-down (REQ-0013-07).
- The keys after the dialog closes go to the canvas: checked with the real session at M1.
- A window resize while the dialog is open changes no field; the dialog's values are of the picture, not of the view.
- The dialog holds nothing between two events on the canvas: the mouse cannot reach the canvas while it is open.

## Out of scope
- A live preview in the dialog; smoothing (Phase 2: a smooth option); remembered values; Black and white (a later feature, JOD's note in the INDEX: decided after this one); rotating by other angles; skewing by a handle.

## Open questions

## Plan
**Approach.** All the math is pure core in a new `src/core/stretch-skew.{c,h}`, libc only; the ui only reads fields and calls it. On the picture OK makes a new buffer and hands the old one to the history, as a quarter turn does. On a selection a stretch is the existing `easel_selection_stretch_oriented`; a skew bakes the shown image into a new lifted image with a mask. There is no preview, so REQ-0025-03 holds by the float's existing path.

**Files.** New: `src/core/stretch-skew.{c,h}`, `src/ui/stretch-skew-dialog.{c,h}`, `tests/core/test-stretch-skew.c`, `tests/perf/perf-0019.c`. Changed: `src/core/selection.{c,h}` (the skew of a float), `src/core/lasso.{c,h}` (a line of several parts), `src/ui/canvas.{c,h}`, `src/ui/window.{c,ui}` and `src/ui/application.c` (action, menus, Ctrl+T), `tests/ui/test-window.c`, `tests/meson.build`, `tests/mutants.txt`.

**Key interfaces (core).**
- `int easel_ss_percent (int size, int pct)`, `int easel_ss_ratio_other (int changed, int from, int other)`: round to the nearest, halves up, at least 1 (REQ-0019-04, -05).
- `int easel_ss_skew_amount (int n, int degrees)`, `int easel_ss_skew_degrees (int s, int n)`, `int easel_ss_shift (int i, int n, int s)`: REQ-0019-10, with the negative rule.
- `void easel_ss_size (int W, int H, int sh, bool sh_degrees, int sv, bool sv_degrees, int *out_w, int *out_h, int *amount_h, int *amount_v)`: the order of REQ-0019-08, with the signed amounts that apply; every field and the New size line use it. `int easel_ss_percent_unlimited (int size, int pct)`: what New size asks for, past the field's limit. `int easel_ss_to_percent (int px, int size)`, `int easel_ss_ratio_fit (int changed, int from, int other)`, `bool easel_ss_too_large (int w, int h)`. `bool easel_ss_make (...)`: the pixels and the shape of a result.
- `EaselTransformResult easel_image_stretch_skew (EaselImage *img, EaselHistory *h, int W, int H, int sh, int sv, uint32_t background, size_t max_bytes)`: `easel_history_push_buffer`, 4 bytes a pixel plus 64.
- `EaselSelectionResult easel_selection_skew (EaselFloat *f, int sh, int sv, size_t max_bytes)` in `selection.c`.
- `bool easel_lasso_outline (const uint8_t *mask, int w, int h, int x, int y, EaselPath *out, size_t max_bytes)`: the grid outline of a mask, one closed line per part, 4-connected, so parts touching at a corner are two. `bool easel_lasso_skew_line (const EaselFloat *f, int amount_h, int amount_v, EaselPath *out, size_t max_bytes)`: the line made before the skew. `const int *easel_path_next_part (const EaselPath *p, size_t *at, size_t *n)`.
- `gboolean easel_canvas_stretch_skew_selection (EaselCanvas *self, int W, int H, int amount_h, int amount_v)` in the ui.

**What grows.** On the picture: the old buffer in the history, as a turn. On a float: the new image and mask, and the hole's shape once it is separated (below), each allocation counted against the lift limit (`sel_lift_max`) as the stretch's are; a total held is never more than the old image, the new image and the hole's mask. A line stores 8 bytes a corner plus one break of 8 bytes per part, under the limit `lasso_*` already has.

**The one function.** Every OK goes through `easel_window`'s `stretch_skew_applied`; what waits is put down at the dialog's opening by `put_down_for_action`, the function Attributes uses, with its flag kept to "leave the selection" as Flip does.

**Readers of what this extends.**
- `EaselPath` gains a break marker between parts (the pair INT_MIN, INT_MIN). Readers to change or check: `lasso.c` (`easel_lasso_scale_path`, `easel_lasso_orient_path`, `easel_lasso_shape`, 9 uses of `xy`), `canvas.c` `append_selection_outline`, `easel_canvas_get_selection_path`, `easel_canvas_transform_selection`, `oriented_line`, `stretch_apply`, `stretch_restore_path`, `end_lasso`, `lasso_begin`, `lasso_extend`, `lasso_free`, `shape_free`. A traced path never has a break, so only the skew's line can.
- `EaselFloat.orig_mask` is read for the hole (10 uses in `selection.c`): a skew must not change the hole. It gains `hole_mask`, set at the first skew from the shape as lifted; readers of the hole read it when it is set.
- `orient`, `orig_px`, `stamp_img`, `kept`, `fill`: a skew completes a lazy image first, keeps the old image for copies as a stretch does (`kept`), then sets `orient` to none and the skewed image as the new original.

**Constitution.** Core stays libc only with bounds and overflow checks on each size; P7 as REQ-0019-17; no ADR. No commit without JOD's word.

**Risks.**
- R1 The break marker reaches a reader that does not expect it. T005 tests each `lasso.c` reader with a two-part line; the ui's in T007.
- R2 `tan` at 89° and rounding differ by platform: `easel_ss_skew_amount` works from a table of the 179 whole degrees' values computed once and checked in T002 against the spec's examples, so the same input gives the same S everywhere (P7.4).
- R3 500 ms at 64 million pixels and 1 s at 100 million may be missed (0018's stretched turn: 443 ms at 64 million). T004 measures right after the core exists. Fallback: rows copied with `memcpy` where a skew has no stretch; if still over, JOD decides the bound.
- R4 `AdwSpinRow` applies typed text at focus-out or `update`, as Attributes does: T006's `as7_too_large` proves Enter with a typed value; a keystroke must not change New size.
- R5 Keys after the dialog closes: manual in T006, since the isolated session does not return the focus as the desktop does.
