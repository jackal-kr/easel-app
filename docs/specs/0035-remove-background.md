# 0035 Remove background

Status: draft
Depends on: 0002, 0005, 0013, 0018, 0019, 0021, 0023, 0034

## Why
A common edit is to cut a subject (a person, a pet, an object) out of its background, for a sticker, a logo or a picture on another
background (JOD, 2026-11-07; `docs/phase2-plan.md` F). Revised 2026-11-09 by JOD's choice of ADR-0007: no learned model (its weights'
licence and training data were not clear, the permissive one too slow and too big; `docs/reference/0035/`), but a classical method that
makes the background transparent by its colors and edges, the same on every machine, with one Tolerance value that starts at an
automatic value and a live preview, so that the user sees and sets what it removes. It does not recognise subjects; its limits are
stated in the dialog and in Help. The model version (implemented and checked, never released) is kept at the end of this file and in
commit 5c1cb53.

## Requirements
Throughout: "removed" means made fully transparent (alpha 0) with the color channels kept; every other pixel is left exactly as it
was (P4: hard edges, no partly transparent pixel is made). "The pixels it looks at" are the whole picture's, or the selection's as
shown (only its shape's for a free-form one, the rest of its box not looked at). "The copy" is the pixels it looks at, at most
2 000 000 of them: themselves when there are no more, else each block of s x s (s the smallest whole number that makes the blocks no
more than 2 000 000) standing for the integer mean of its pixels that are looked at and not fully transparent, a block with none of
those standing for no pixel. "The ring" is the copy's outermost d rows and columns, d the copy's shorter side divided by 100 and
rounded down, at least 2, and each of its cells with one of its four neighbours standing for no pixel. Colors are compared by their
distance in an integer approximation of CIE Lab (D65), in delta-E.

A marked selection is lifted when the card opens (if the lift cannot get its memory, the message of REQ-0035-06, the selection stays marked and no card opens); a floating one is acted on as shown, as REQ-0034-03 says for Black and White (the result becomes its pixels at its shown size with no flip or turn left over; a pasted one's fully transparent pixels stay not put down); only the pixels it looks at can be removed; at OK the result floats and joins the selection's one undo step. (Before: no dialog; a marked selection was lifted and the work began at once, its result floating as soon as it was made.)
- REQ-0035-03 The app shall remove the background by its colors and edges, with no model, as ADR-0007 sets out and `docs/reference/0035/classical/classical-v3.c` does for a whole opaque picture (the seam of (b) added since): (a) the background is described twice: as a colour line, the ray from black through the ring's median color (one color under more or less light, a glow or a vignette), reaching along it as far as the ring's colors within three times their median distance go, a quarter of that range farther toward darker and four times it toward brighter; and as a smooth surface per Lab channel, a polynomial of degree 3 in x and y fitted by least squares to the ring's cells on the line (within three times its median distance), then three times refitted without the cells farther than three times the median distance (at least 1 delta-E), once when the analysis is made, not again when the Tolerance changes; and as up to six color clusters of the ring (k-means from the farthest points, 12 rounds, each holding 10 % of the ring or more), used only where they explain the ring better than the surface (their median distance less: a backdrop of several colors, stripes, a horizon); a cell's distance to the background is to the surface, to the line when the cell is colored (Lab chroma 12 or more) and the line explains the ring at least as well as the surface (its median distance no larger), or to the nearest cluster when they are used, whichever is least, the line's distance taken in Lab from its nearest point (2026-11-10, before: the surface and the line only; a backdrop of two colors or stripes kept half of itself); (b) background seeds are the ring's cells within the tolerance of the background, subject seeds the copy's cells at least four tolerances from it, and each other cell takes, in order of its edge strength (the sum over L, a and b of the right-minus-left and below-minus-above differences, lowest first, first come first served among equals), the label of the neighbour that reached it, and then every subject cell beside a background cell whose color is within the tolerance of the surface becomes background (the seam: a step's two sides have the same edge strength, so without this a one-pixel fringe of background stays round the subject; all such cells are found before any changes); (c) of what is labeled subject, the largest 4-connected region is kept with any other at least a tenth of its size, the rest becoming background, and enclosed background regions (touching no edge of the copy) under 1 % of its cells become subject; (d) at full size a pixel takes the label of its block when that block and its eight neighbours agree, and otherwise that of the neighbouring block, of those nine, whose color is nearest its own (ties: subject); (e) a fully transparent pixel is background from the start, stays as it is, and is no seed; (f) every background pixel that is not fully transparent is removed; (g) then the fringe: for each of the four directions in which a kept pixel has a removed neighbour on one side and a kept one on the other, the subject's color is taken inward where the color has settled (each step of the walk more than half the tolerance in delta-E, at most 16 pixels) and the background's outward the same way over removed pixels; the run of kept pixels inward from it whose RGB is a blend of the two, the background's share above one half and within an eighth of the two colors' distance from their line, is removed, in one round decided before any pixel of it is removed (JOD asked for rounds while one removes, at most 3 or 4; a second round judged against the blends the first removed and bit into the subject: `docs/reference/0035/methods-comparison.md`); a line one pixel wide has no kept pixel inward and stays. The same pixels and Tolerance give the same pixels on every machine. (Before 2026-11-10: (a) was the surface alone, fitted to every ring cell and refitted once without those farther than twice the first estimate, and again for each Tolerance; a glow of the backdrop's color stayed, and a subject crossing the ring pulled the surface (JOD's green-backdrop portrait); before: a model found "every main subject"; then, until 2026-11-10, no (g): a fringe of mixed pixels stayed round soft edges, JOD's eagle photo.)

## Edge cases
- A picture with partly transparent pixels: judged by their colors as any other and kept or removed whole; none is made partly transparent.
- A subject that touches the picture's edge: where it crosses the ring it is far from the surface and seeds the subject; up to half of the ring may be subject without raising the automatic tolerance (the median).
- A picture of one color: the surface is that color, no subject seed: all of it is background, OK removes it all, one step.
- A copy whose ring has no cell (a picture all fully transparent, or a selection's shape too thin): no surface, nothing removed.
- A long, thin picture (8000 × 16): d is 2, the method as any other.
- Saving afterwards: PNG, TIFF and WebP keep the transparency, JPEG and BMP flatten it on white (REQ-0005-11), nothing new.
- The selection moved partly outside the picture: the selection as shown is looked at, its off-picture part too.
- A hole through the subject that shows the background (the middle of a ring, the gap under an arm): no background seed reaches it, so it stays; the user selects it and removes it alone.
- A glow that fades toward a paler, greyer color than the backdrop (not more light on the same color): its color is not the
  backdrop's, so it stays; the user clicks it away (Remove Background: pieces) or raises the Tolerance (a documented limit, 2026-11-10).
- A subject that loses itself gradually as the Tolerance rises (a face shading into a beard and a dark shirt on the border): the check compares each step with 4, not with the step before, so the slow loss is caught (2026-11-10, T005).
- A grainy or busy backdrop (since the stability check, 2026-11-10): the subject is kept with much of the backdrop instead of removed with all of it; the hint shows.
- (Was a limit before the robust fit, 2026-11-10: a wallpaper whose border fit as poorly as picture 04's but needed about twice its
  Tolerance, the mug; with the robust fit its median is 0.39 delta-E and it comes out at the automatic 5, and picture 04's median is
  0.63, far from the knee of the automatic rule.)
- A strongly textured backdrop (grain, blotches): its median distance to the surface is high, so the automatic tolerance is high (up to 20) and a subject of moderate contrast can be lost whole; at Tolerance 10 to 12 it is right (a documented limit, 2026-11-10, the held-out textured picture).
- A sky with soft white clouds, a white backdrop with a soft shadow: part of the clouds, part of the shadow stay (95 % of the background removed); a click on them removes them (Remove Background: pieces).
- A subject partly in the backdrop's own color (a shirt partly the green of the backdrop): that part goes with the background where the ring's clusters judge; a press on it brings it back (Remove Background: pieces).
- Subjects in a darker or lighter shade of the backdrop's own color (a darker-blue animal in blue water, line art in a shade of a gradient's colors): the colour line does not judge where the surface explains the ring better, so they stay as before; where the line judges, such a subject goes with the background.
- Line art over a horizon (the blind benchmark's composite): the ring's clusters judge, so the automatic tolerance is lower, and the thin lines go with the ground's colour; Tolerance a step higher, a click or Keep this brings them back (a known limit, 2026-11-10).
- A grainy backdrop of one hue (grey grain): the clusters may explain the ring better than the surface, so the automatic tolerance stays low (5) where it was 20 before (2026-11-10).
- Remove Background twice: the removed background is fully transparent, background from the start; the second finds what is left.
- The dialog open and the window resized: the preview stays as it is; nothing else takes input.

## Out of scope
- A model, subject recognition, choosing the subject by a click, soft edges (decided: hard), replacing the background with a color or a picture, a second setting, the "Keep this" and "Remove this" brushes (`docs/phase2-plan.md` G, later), GrabCut or any iterated graph cut (ADR-0007).

### Requirements, scenarios and edge cases of the model version (verbatim)
Throughout: "removed" means made fully transparent (alpha 0) with the color channels kept; every other pixel is left exactly as it
was (P4: hard edges, no partly transparent pixel is made). "The pixels it looks at" are the whole picture's, or the selection's as
shown (only its shape's for a free-form one, the rest of its box not looked at).

#### The command
- (was) REQ-0035-01 (Adds to REQ-0018-01, -02.) The Image menu shall hold Remove Background right after Black and White…, with no shortcut; the selection menu (REQ-0013-17) holds it right after its Black and White…. It follows REQ-0018-01's rules for when an item acts. Its strings (the item, the progress dialog's heading and Cancel, the headings and bodies of REQ-0035-05 and -06) are translated in every language of 0023, the item's mnemonic differing from the others of its menu.
- (was) REQ-0035-02 Choosing it shall first do what an Image action does to what waits (REQ-0018-03): a pending curve, polygon or gradient and an open text box are put down, and stay put down whatever follows; a text box whose put-down cannot get its memory stays open and nothing more happens. Then it shall act on the selection while one exists, and on the whole picture otherwise. A marked selection is lifted first (if the lift cannot get its memory, REQ-0013-19's dialog and the selection stays marked); a floating one is acted on as shown, as REQ-0034-03 says for Black and White (the result becomes its pixels at its shown size with no flip or turn left over; a pasted one's fully transparent pixels stay not put down); only the pixels it looks at can be removed; the result floats and joins the selection's one undo step. - (was) REQ-0035-03 The app shall find every main subject in the pixels it looks at (people, animals, objects: what a person would call the main things, a person and their dog both) and remove every pixel it judges background, with no setting. #### Waiting
- (was) REQ-0035-04 If the work takes longer than 0.5 s, the app shall show a modal dialog with the heading "Removing the Background", a progress bar (showing the share done where it is known, pulsing where it is not, moving at least once a second) and Cancel. - (was) REQ-0035-05 If no pixel would change (no subject is found, everything is judged subject, or the background is already transparent), the app shall show a dialog with the heading "Nothing to Remove" and the body "No background was found to remove.", change nothing, add no step and no modified mark; a marked selection it lifted is marked again (unlike REQ-0034-03, since nothing happened and the dialog says so; JOD, 2026-11-09). #### Edge cases
- A picture with partly transparent pixels: judged as any other and kept or removed whole; none is made partly transparent.
- A subject that touches the picture's edges: kept as found; the edge does not count as background.
- A long, thin picture (8000 × 16): looked at as any other; narrower than 16 pixels gives REQ-0035-05 at once.
- Saving afterwards: PNG, TIFF and WebP keep the transparency, JPEG and BMP flatten it on white (REQ-0005-11), nothing new.
- The selection moved partly outside the picture: the selection as shown is looked at, its off-picture part too.
- Remove Background twice: the second removes nothing more and gives REQ-0035-05.
- Cancel while the model loads, the first run after start (JOD, 2026-11-09, at the check): the loading cannot be stopped, so the work ends when it has loaded, 0.2 s on the dev machine and up to 1 s in the Flatpak (T001's probe); nothing is applied, as for any Cancel. Before: REQ-0035-04's "within 0.5 s" read as if for every moment of the work; now that half second holds once the model is loaded, and this one wait is a documented limit.
