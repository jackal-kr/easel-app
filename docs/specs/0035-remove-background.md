# 0035 Remove background

Status: draft
Depends on: 0002, 0005, 0013, 0018, 0019, 0021, 0023, 0034

## Why
A common edit is to cut a subject (a person, a pet, an object) out of its background, for a sticker, a logo or a picture on another
background (JOD, 2026-11-07; `docs/phase2-plan.md` F). Revised 2026-11-09 by JOD's choice of ADR-0007: no learned model (its weights'
licence and training data were not clear, the permissive one too slow and too big; `docs/reference/0035/`), but a classical method that
makes the background transparent by its colors and edges, the same on every machine, with one Tolerance value that starts at an
automatic value and a live preview, so that the user sees and sets what it removes. It does not recognise subjects; its limits are
stated in the dialog and in Help. The model version (implemented and checked, never released) is kept at the end of this file and in
commit 5c1cb53.

## Requirements
Throughout: "removed" means made fully transparent (alpha 0) with the color channels kept; every other pixel is left exactly as it
was (P4: hard edges, no partly transparent pixel is made). "The pixels it looks at" are the whole picture's, or the selection's as
shown (only its shape's for a free-form one, the rest of its box not looked at). "The copy" is the pixels it looks at, at most
2 000 000 of them: themselves when there are no more, else each block of s x s (s the smallest whole number that makes the blocks no
more than 2 000 000) standing for the integer mean of its pixels that are looked at and not fully transparent, a block with none of
those standing for no pixel. "The ring" is the copy's outermost d rows and columns, d the copy's shorter side divided by 100 and
rounded down, at least 2, and each of its cells with one of its four neighbours standing for no pixel. Colors are compared by their
distance in an integer approximation of CIE Lab (D65), in delta-E.

- REQ-0035-01 (Adds to REQ-0018-01, -02.) The Image menu shall hold Remove Background… right after Black and White…, with no shortcut; the selection menu (REQ-0013-17) holds it right after its Black and White…; its label ends in an ellipsis, as every item that asks for more before it acts (JOD, 2026-11-10; before: "Remove Background" with none). It follows REQ-0018-01's rules for when an item acts, and is always available while they allow it. Its strings (the item, the card's heading, line, Tolerance and buttons, the progress dialog's heading, the failure's heading and body) are translated in every language of 0023, the item's mnemonic differing from the others of its menu. (Before: no dialog of its own, the strings of REQ-0035-05's message and of "Background removal is not available.", and a greyed copy of the item in a build without the model, REQ-0035-10.)
- REQ-0035-02 Choosing it shall first do what an Image action does to what waits (REQ-0018-03): a pending curve, polygon or gradient and an open text box are put down, and stay put down whatever follows; a text box whose put-down cannot get its memory stays open and nothing more happens. Then it opens the card of REQ-0035-04 for the selection while one exists, and for the whole picture otherwise. A marked selection is lifted when the card opens (if the lift cannot get its memory, the message of REQ-0035-06, the selection stays marked and no card opens); a floating one is acted on as shown, as REQ-0034-03 says for Black and White (the result becomes its pixels at its shown size with no flip or turn left over; a pasted one's fully transparent pixels stay not put down); only the pixels it looks at can be removed; at OK the result floats and joins the selection's one undo step. On the whole picture OK makes one undo step that costs what REQ-0002-16 allows for the pixels it changes (a step larger than the budget is kept as the only one, REQ-0002-17). (Before: no dialog; a marked selection was lifted and the work began at once, its result floating as soon as it was made.)
- REQ-0035-03 The app shall remove the background by its colors and edges, with no model, as ADR-0007 sets out and `docs/reference/0035/classical/classical-v3.c` does for a whole opaque picture (the seam of (b) added since): (a) the background is a smooth surface per Lab channel, a polynomial of degree 3 in x and y fitted by least squares to the ring's cells, then fitted again without the cells farther from the first fit than twice the tolerance; (b) background seeds are the ring's cells within the tolerance of the surface, subject seeds the copy's cells at least four tolerances from it, and each other cell takes, in order of its edge strength (the sum over L, a and b of the right-minus-left and below-minus-above differences, lowest first, first come first served among equals), the label of the neighbour that reached it, and then every subject cell beside a background cell whose color is within the tolerance of the surface becomes background (the seam: a step's two sides have the same edge strength, so without this a one-pixel fringe of background stays round the subject; all such cells are found before any changes); (c) of what is labeled subject, the largest 4-connected region is kept with any other at least a tenth of its size, the rest becoming background, and enclosed background regions (touching no edge of the copy) under 1 % of its cells become subject; (d) at full size a pixel takes the label of its block when that block and its eight neighbours agree, and otherwise that of the neighbouring block, of those nine, whose color is nearest its own (ties: subject); (e) a fully transparent pixel is background from the start, stays as it is, and is no seed; (f) every background pixel that is not fully transparent is removed; (g) then the fringe: for each of the four directions in which a kept pixel has a removed neighbour on one side and a kept one on the other, the subject's color is taken inward where the color has settled (each step of the walk more than half the tolerance in delta-E, at most 16 pixels) and the background's outward the same way over removed pixels; the run of kept pixels inward from it whose RGB is a blend of the two, the background's share above one half and within an eighth of the two colors' distance from their line, is removed, in one round decided before any pixel of it is removed (JOD asked for rounds while one removes, at most 3 or 4; a second round judged against the blends the first removed and bit into the subject: `docs/reference/0035/methods-comparison.md`); a line one pixel wide has no kept pixel inward and stays. The same pixels and Tolerance give the same pixels on every machine. (Before: a model found "every main subject"; then, until 2026-11-10, no (g): a fringe of mixed pixels stayed round soft edges, JOD's eagle photo.)

## Edge cases
- A picture with partly transparent pixels: judged by their colors as any other and kept or removed whole; none is made partly transparent.
- A subject that touches the picture's edge: where it crosses the ring it is far from the surface and seeds the subject; up to half of the ring may be subject without raising the automatic tolerance (the median).
- A picture of one color: the surface is that color, no subject seed: all of it is background, OK removes it all, one step.
- A copy whose ring has no cell (a picture all fully transparent, or a selection's shape too thin): no surface, nothing removed.
- A long, thin picture (8000 × 16): d is 2, the method as any other.
- Saving afterwards: PNG, TIFF and WebP keep the transparency, JPEG and BMP flatten it on white (REQ-0005-11), nothing new.
- The selection moved partly outside the picture: the selection as shown is looked at, its off-picture part too.
- A hole through the subject that shows the background (the middle of a ring, the gap under an arm): no background seed reaches it, so it stays; the user selects it and removes it alone.
- Remove Background twice: the removed background is fully transparent, background from the start; the second finds what is left.
- The dialog open and the window resized: the preview stays as it is; nothing else takes input.

## Out of scope
- A model, subject recognition, choosing the subject by a click, soft edges (decided: hard), replacing the background with a color or a picture, a second setting, the "Keep this" and "Remove this" brushes (`docs/phase2-plan.md` G, later), GrabCut or any iterated graph cut (ADR-0007).

The Image menu shall hold Remove Background right after Black and White…, with no shortcut; the selection menu (REQ-0013-17) holds it right after its Black and White…. It follows REQ-0018-01's rules for when an item acts. Its strings (the item, the progress dialog's heading and Cancel, the headings and bodies of REQ-0035-05 and -06) are translated in every language of 0023, the item's mnemonic differing from the others of its menu.
- (was) REQ-0035-02 Choosing it shall first do what an Image action does to what waits (REQ-0018-03): a pending curve, polygon or gradient and an open text box are put down, and stay put down whatever follows; a text box whose put-down cannot get its memory stays open and nothing more happens. Then it shall act on the selection while one exists, and on the whole picture otherwise. A marked selection is lifted first (if the lift cannot get its memory, REQ-0013-19's dialog and the selection stays marked); a floating one is acted on as shown, as REQ-0034-03 says for Black and White (the result becomes its pixels at its shown size with no flip or turn left over; a pasted one's fully transparent pixels stay not put down); only the pixels it looks at can be removed; the result floats and joins the selection's one undo step. On the whole picture it is one undo step that costs what REQ-0002-16 allows for the pixels it changes (a step larger than the budget is kept as the only one, REQ-0002-17).
- (was) REQ-0035-03 The app shall find every main subject in the pixels it looks at (people, animals, objects: what a person would call the main things, a person and their dog both) and remove every pixel it judges background, with no setting. A pixel already fully transparent counts as background and stays as it is.

#### Waiting
- (was) REQ-0035-04 If the work takes longer than 0.5 s, the app shall show a modal dialog with the heading "Removing the Background", a progress bar (showing the share done where it is known, pulsing where it is not, moving at least once a second) and Cancel. From the choice of the command until the work ends, the window takes no input except through that dialog: the picture, the selection, the tool, the colors and the settings stay as they were when it was chosen. Cancel, Esc, Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+W or a close request stop the work within 0.5 s: nothing of the removal is applied and no step is added for it; what REQ-0035-02 put down stays put down; a selection is as before the command, marked again if the command lifted it; a close request then goes on as REQ-0001-34 and REQ-0001-35 say (the Save prompt answered Cancel leaves the window with the picture as before and starts nothing again). A Cancel that comes after the result is shown does nothing; Undo is then the way back. The dialog closes by itself when the work ends.

#### When nothing is removed
- (was) REQ-0035-05 If no pixel would change (no subject is found, everything is judged subject, or the background is already transparent), the app shall show a dialog with the heading "Nothing to Remove" and the body "No background was found to remove.", change nothing, add no step and no modified mark; a marked selection it lifted is marked again (unlike REQ-0034-03, since nothing happened and the dialog says so; JOD, 2026-11-09). Pixels that all have one color (all four channels the same), or that are narrower or shorter than 16 pixels, give this dialog at once, without the subject being looked for.

#### Failures
- (was) REQ-0035-06 If the memory it needs cannot be had, at whatever point of the work, the app shall show the heading "Could Not Remove the Background" with the body "There is not enough memory for this change." and change nothing of the removal (what was put down stays put down; a selection as before the command, marked again if the command lifted it). If the model that finds the subject is there at start but cannot be loaded or fails while it runs (one missing at start: REQ-0035-10), the same heading with the body "Background removal is not available." and nothing changed in the same way. Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+W or a close request while one of these dialogs or REQ-0035-05's is open closes it, then goes on as REQ-0001-34 and REQ-0001-35 say (as REQ-0021-03).

#### The pixel contract and cost
- (was) REQ-0035-07 (P7, with an exception to P7.4 in the model's ADR and the constitution.) Kept pixels are exactly as they were and removed ones keep their color with alpha 0 (P7.1); a floating selection shows its result one to one (P7.3). The same pixels give the same result every time on the same machine with the same build, in any process; between machines the result may differ along the subject's edge (JOD's expectation: a few pixels), with no bound promised or tested.
- (was) REQ-0035-08 It shall work with no network (the Flatpak has none, REQ-0024-10).
- (was) REQ-0035-09 Its time, from the choice of the command to the result painted, the first run after the app starts (the model's loading) and a later run each, for an 8000 × 8000 and a 1920 × 1080 picture, and the memory it needs beyond the picture and its undo step, shall be within the budgets JOD accepts in the model's ADR, added to `docs/engineering.md`'s budgets (ADR-0006, 2026-11-09: 2 s for 1920 × 1080, 3 s for 8000 × 8000, 2 GB of memory).
- (was) REQ-0035-10 (JOD, 2026-11-09, at the Plan; adds to REQ-0035-01.) The app shall build and run without the model's runtime or the model (a build that leaves them out, or an installation where the model is missing at start): Remove Background is then still in both menus, in its place, not available (greyed), its label "Remove Background (Not in This Copy)", and every other part of the app works as before. Before: REQ-0035-01 had the item always available, a missing model being only REQ-0035-06's error when chosen; now that error is for a model that is there at start and cannot be loaded or fails while it runs.

#### Edge cases
- A picture with partly transparent pixels: judged as any other and kept or removed whole; none is made partly transparent.
- A subject that touches the picture's edges: kept as found; the edge does not count as background.
- A long, thin picture (8000 × 16): looked at as any other; narrower than 16 pixels gives REQ-0035-05 at once.
- Saving afterwards: PNG, TIFF and WebP keep the transparency, JPEG and BMP flatten it on white (REQ-0005-11), nothing new.
- The selection moved partly outside the picture: the selection as shown is looked at, its off-picture part too.
- Remove Background twice: the second removes nothing more and gives REQ-0035-05.
- Cancel while the model loads, the first run after start (JOD, 2026-11-09, at the check): the loading cannot be stopped, so the work ends when it has loaded, 0.2 s on the dev machine and up to 1 s in the Flatpak (T001's probe); nothing is applied, as for any Cancel. Before: REQ-0035-04's "within 0.5 s" read as if for every moment of the work; now that half second holds once the model is loaded, and this one wait is a documented limit.
