# 0042 Invert selection

Status: implemented
Reviewed: spec-reviewer, 2026-11-11 (9 findings merged; the memory failure decided routinely as 0014's dialog)
Depends on: 0013, 0014, 0021, 0023, 0041

## Why
To work on everything except a part of the picture (clear the background around a person, recolor all but a logo) the user would have
to select the surroundings piece by piece. Invert Selection selects everything outside the current selection in one command; it is a
modern extra (`docs/product.md`, `docs/reference/new-behaviors.md`). It changes no pixel (unlike Invert Colors, REQ-0018-05).

## Requirements
- REQ-0042-01 The primary menu's Edit submenu (REQ-0013-16) shall have "In_vert Selection" directly after "Select _All", and a selection's context menu (REQ-0013-17) the same item directly after its "Select _All", both with the shortcut Ctrl+Shift+J (JOD, 2026-11-11: free in Easel and in the desktop; Ctrl+Shift+I opens GTK's inspector where its keybinding is on), shown beside the item, in the Shortcuts window and in Help. The item and its key are sensitive only while a selection exists (marked or floating, Select or Free-Form Select). The key does nothing where REQ-0021-01's keys do nothing (before the picture exists, with a dialog, the quit prompt, the color chooser or a menu open, a button held in the canvas area, the selection or a resize handle being dragged, the focus in a text field of the font card); the item acts from its own menu.
- REQ-0042-02 When the user inverts a marked selection, the app shall make the selection every pixel of the picture that was not selected, and only those: for a rectangle, the picture less the rectangle; for a Free-Form shape, the picture less the shape. The new selection is marked (nothing is lifted), Free-Form Select becomes the active tool and keeps it (JOD, 2026-11-11; REQ-0014-10 does not apply to this switch), and from then on it is a Free-Form selection in every respect (REQ-0014-05 to REQ-0014-17: moved, copied, cleared, cut, flipped, stretched and cropped as one).
- REQ-0042-03 When the user inverts a floating selection, the app shall first put it down where it is, as REQ-0013-11 says for it (a moved one in the step of its lift; a pasted one as REQ-0016-05 says; JOD, 2026-11-11), then select every pixel of the picture outside its whole shape (its rectangle for a rectangular one; background-colored pixels included, as REQ-0013-13 keeps them in both modes), as REQ-0042-02. If the put-down fails (REQ-0013-19), nothing is inverted (JOD, 2026-11-12).
- REQ-0042-04 Inverting adds no undo step and changes no pixel, apart from a floating selection's put-down (REQ-0042-03); it does not mark the picture modified. Undo after it takes back the edit before it and leaves no selection, the tool staying Free-Form Select. Inverting twice selects the first selection's pixels again (as a Free-Form selection).
- REQ-0042-05 If the inverted selection would have no pixel (the selection, marked, floating or Free-Form, covered the whole picture), then the app shall remove the selection (after a floating one's put-down): nothing is selected and the tool stays as it was.
- REQ-0042-06 A selection's shape may have holes and several separate pieces (a hole when a rectangle inside the picture is inverted; pieces when a band across it is). It shall be outlined along every grid edge between a selected pixel and a pixel that is unselected or outside the picture, in REQ-0014-05's line and dashes; each closed boundary is one dashed line, as a skewed selection's lines are (REQ-0019-12): an outer boundary counted from its corner of smallest y, then smallest x, clockwise; a hole's counted counter-clockwise from the first edge of its boundary in the order of smallest y, then smallest x, along which the selection lies below it (for a rectangular hole its bottom-left corner), so that the selection is always on the line's right (JOD, 2026-11-12). The status bar shows the size of its bounding rectangle (REQ-0014-07).
- REQ-0042-07 If the memory for the new selection's shape cannot be had, then the app shall show the dialog of REQ-0014-15 ("Could Not Make the Selection") and leave the selection as it was: a marked one marked, a floating one still floating where it was, nothing put down.

## Edge cases
- A selection of one pixel: the inverted one is every other pixel.
- A floating selection partly outside the picture: put down (the part outside is lost, REQ-0013-11), then the rest of the picture is selected.
- A Free-Form selection whose shape touches the picture's edges: its inverse has no line along those edges where it has no pixel.
- Invert while a resize handle or the selection is dragged: ignored (REQ-0042-01).

## Out of scope
- Selecting by color (a magic wand), adding to or subtracting from a selection.

## Plan
(Opus, 2026-11-12.) Read with `docs/engineering.md`, `docs/design.md`, 0013, 0014 (the line, the shape), 0015, 0018, 0019 (a shape's outline, REQ-0019-12) and 0041's Plan (a core rule, its action, its menus).

Approach.
- The new selection is made of two things that already exist: a mask (the shape) and the line along its grid edges. The line is `easel_lasso_outline` (0019 made it for a skewed shape: one closed line for each part, a line of its own for each hole, `EASEL_PATH_BREAK` between them), so a Free-Form selection built from them behaves in every respect as 0014's: its press, its move, its stretch, its flips, its Clear, Cut, Copy and Crop read the mask and the line already.
- One core function makes the shape: the pixels of the picture that are not in the selection, as a mask over their bounding rectangle (REQ-0014-07), with no allocation as big as the picture besides the mask itself. The selected pixels are counted per picture row and per picture column (4 bytes each, so a few tens of KB), the bounding rectangle is the first and last row and column that a selected pixel does not fill, and the mask is written once at that size. Only the part of the selection inside the picture counts (a floating one may hang past an edge); a rectangle is the same with no mask. No pixel left: NOTHING (REQ-0042-05).
- One canvas function does the rest: it makes the shape and its line first (so that a refusal changes nothing, REQ-0042-07), then, for a floating selection, puts it down by `finish_selection` (the one function every put-down goes through; REQ-0013-11, REQ-0016-05), then installs the new selection as a marked Free-Form one: `sel_mask`, `sel_path` (the outline), its rectangle, `sel_state` 1, as `end_lasso` does. It adds no history step (the put-down has its own). Inverting twice is the same function on a mask.
- The window's action calls it with the gates of Stretch and Skew and of Crop to Selection (`view_actions_allowed`, panning, the font card's field), then makes Free-Form Select the tool without putting the selection down: the tool's change handler (`change_tool_state`) puts down what another tool leaves (REQ-0014-10), so it gets a flag, set around the one call that Invert Selection makes, that skips that. A selection that came out empty leaves the tool as it was.
- The item is sensitive with the clipboard's selection actions (`update_clipboard_actions`), as Crop's is. Strings: "In_vert Selection" in the Edit submenu and the selection's menu after Select All, Ctrl+Shift+J, a row of the Shortcuts window, the Help pages, eleven translations with mnemonics the menu test accepts.

Files and interfaces.
- `src/core/selection.{c,h}`: `EaselSelectionResult easel_selection_invert (const uint8_t *mask, int sx, int sy, int sw, int sh, int img_w, int img_h, int *x, int *y, int *w, int *h, uint8_t **out, size_t max_bytes)`: the box (sx, sy, sw, sh) of the selection (anywhere, also past the picture's edges) and its mask (NULL: every pixel of the box); DONE with the bounding rectangle and `*out` (w x h bytes, 1 for a pixel of the new shape, the caller frees it); NOTHING with no mask when every pixel of the picture is selected; NO_MEMORY, nothing made, when an allocation (the counts, the mask), each counted by itself, is above max_bytes or cannot be had.
- `src/ui/canvas.{c,h}`: `EaselSelectionResult easel_canvas_invert_selection (EaselCanvas *self, gboolean *selected)`: NOTHING and no change without a selection or with a button held; DONE, `*selected` FALSE when nothing is left (the selection removed, a floating one put down); NO_MEMORY, the selection as it was, after `selection-failed` with kind 2 (REQ-0014-15's dialog), the limit `sel_lift_max` that the lasso's shape uses.
- `src/ui/window.{c,ui}`: the action `invert-selection`, `invert_selection_activated`, the flag in `change_tool_state`, the two menu items, the sensitivity; `src/ui/application.c` the accelerator; `src/ui/help.c` the row; `data/help/*/index.html` twelve rows; `po/` two messages in eleven languages.

What grows. The counts (4 bytes a row and a column of the picture), the mask (at most the picture's pixels, a byte each), the outline's own work (`easel_lasso_outline` makes a byte per pixel of the mask for its marks, and the line at 8 bytes a corner): each counted against `sel_lift_max` as one allocation, as the lasso's shape is; the selection keeps the mask and the line, which a Free-Form selection does already. Nothing goes to the history.

Risks.
1. REQ-0042-06's "each closed boundary ... from its corner of smallest y, then smallest x, going clockwise" is not what `easel_lasso_outline` does for a hole: checked by a port of it (a 10 x 8 picture less a 4 x 4 rectangle): the outer line (0, 0) (10, 0) (10, 8) (0, 8), the hole's line (3, 6) (7, 6) (7, 2) (3, 2): from the hole's bottom-left corner, counter-clockwise, that is with the selection always on its right, as every skewed selection's lines are (REQ-0019-12). Only the dashes' starting point on a hole's line differs. Changing the function would change 0019's lines; the wording is the one to change: a spec gap for JOD below.
2. The tool change: `change_tool_state` puts the selection down for another tool; the flag must be set only around Invert Selection's own call and cleared whatever happens. Checked by a ui test through the action, with Select the tool and with Free-Form Select already the tool, and by a mutant for each branch.
3. A floating selection whose put-down then fails (REQ-0013-19: its own dialog, the picture as before the lift, the selection gone): the new shape is thrown away and nothing is inverted; tested with the step limit as 0041's `as6_whole_float_put_down_fails` does. The spec does not say it: a gap for JOD below.
4. The line of an inverted selection after a stretch is the traced line scaled (REQ-0015-06), as for any Free-Form selection, so it may stop following the mask's edges exactly; the shape and every pixel are right. A ui case (a stretched inverted selection: the mask scaled, the part counted) and a render probe, in the first ui task, that the outline's several parts are drawn as they are for a skewed float.
5.
