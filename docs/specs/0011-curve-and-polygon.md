# 0011 Curve and Polygon

Status: implemented
Depends on: 0001, 0002, 0003, 0004, 0006, 0007, 0010

## Why
XP Paint's two multi-click tools: a curve (a line bent once or twice) and a polygon (corners clicked one after another),
which complete the line-and-shape tools of 0010. Unlike them they wait between clicks, so they bring the app's first
"pending" shape (`docs/design.md`, Pending state): shown, not yet in the picture, put down or dropped as one step.
Decisions by JOD on 2026-10-19: the first drag's button decides the color and the other button cancels; XP's bends; a
polygon closes by a double-click, a release near its first corner, or Enter; the alternate fill rule; an unfinished one is
put down by what changes the picture or its file, dropped by Esc and Undo, and redrawn when the width or fill style changes;
a thin curve with exact pixels; a double-click that closes only within its distance; Enter ignored while a button is held.

## Requirements
### The tools and their options
- REQ-0011-01 The toolbox shall show Curve and Polygon, with the tooltips "Curve" and "Polygon", in their places of the final order of REQ-0006-01, so that the toolbox is, in two columns, row by row: Pan, Magnifier / Eraser, Pick Color / Fill, Pencil / Brush, Airbrush / Line, Curve / Rectangle, Rounded Rectangle / Ellipse, Polygon. Clicking one makes it the active tool (REQ-0001-09). (0012 adds Gradient after Fill, which moves Pencil and the tools after it one place on: REQ-0012-01.)
- REQ-0011-02 While Curve is active, the options panel shall show the five line widths as for Line; while Polygon is active, the fill styles, a line and the five widths as for the three shapes (REQ-0010-02). The width is the one shared by Line, the shapes, Curve and Polygon, and the fill style the one shared by the shapes and Polygon (REQ-0010-03); Numpad + and − step the width (REQ-0010-04).
- REQ-0011-03 The status bar hint (REQ-0004-09) shall be, for Curve: "Draws a curved line with the selected line width."; for Polygon: "Draws a polygon with the selected fill style." The cursor is the crosshair of REQ-0010-15.

### Drawing a curve
- REQ-0011-04 A curve shall be made in three stages, each a press, an optional drag and a release of the same button in the canvas area: the line, then the first bend, then the second bend. The line is shown as Line draws it (REQ-0010-05, REQ-0010-10), from the pixel under the press to the pixel under the pointer, Shift constraining it (REQ-0010-14). The first bend puts both control points of the curve at the pixel under the pointer; the second bend moves only the second control point to the pixel under the pointer; Shift has no effect on the bends. While a stage's button is held the curve follows the pointer; its release fixes that stage. The release of the second bend puts the curve down.
- REQ-0011-05 The curve shall be the cubic Bézier curve from the start pixel through its two control points to the end pixel, all taken at pixel centers. Before the first bend, and whenever both control points lie on the closed segment between the start's and the end's centers, its pixels are exactly the Line between its ends (REQ-0010-10). Otherwise its 1-pixel path is made from N + 1 points of the curve at equal steps of its parameter, from the start (0) to the end (1), where N is the length in pixels of the path start, first control point, second control point, end, rounded up (at least 1): each point gives the pixel whose center is nearest (a half rounded up, toward larger x or y), repeated pixels in a row count once, consecutive pixels are joined by the Pencil's line (REQ-0001-20), and then, along the path, a pixel is left out when the pixel kept before it and the pixel after it touch (horizontally, vertically or diagonally), so the path has no L-shaped corners. With a width w above 1, every pixel of the path is stamped with the w × w square of REQ-0010-10. The same start, end, control points and width always give the same pixels, in the preview and in the picture.

### Drawing a polygon
- REQ-0011-06 A polygon shall be made of sides, each a press, an optional drag and a release of the same button in the canvas area: the first shows its first side from the pixel under the press (the first corner) to the pixel under the pointer, and its release adds that pixel as the second corner, also when it is near the first; after that each press shows the next side from the last corner to the pixel under the pointer, following it while the button is held, and its release adds the side's end as shown as the next corner. Shift constrains the side being dragged as it constrains a Line (REQ-0010-14). While no button is held, only the sides already placed are shown, and no side follows the pointer.
- REQ-0011-07 From the second side on, the polygon shall close, with a last side from the last corner back to the first, and be put down, when: the release of a side ends with the pointer at a straight-line distance of at most 4 logical pixels from the first corner's center on screen, at any zoom (that side adds no corner); the second press of a double-click (a press of the same button within the desktop's double-click time and distance of the previous click) is released within the double-click distance of that press (the corner of the previous click is the last; released farther away, it adds a corner as any side does); or the user presses Enter (keypad Enter too). Further presses of the same multi-click (a third click of a triple-click) after the polygon was put down are ignored, with their releases.
- REQ-0011-08 The polygon's pixels shall follow the rules of the shapes (REQ-0010-12, REQ-0010-13) with these definitions: its outline is the Lines of its sides with the width w (REQ-0010-10), the closing side included; its region is the pixels whose center lies inside the polygon by the alternate rule, the corners taken at pixel centers: a center is inside when the sides crossing the horizontal line through it to its right are odd in number, a side crossing that line when one of its ends has a larger y than the center (is lower on the screen) and the other has not, and a center that lies on a side is inside; its inside is its region less its outline. "Outline" draws the outline; "Outline and fill" the outline in the drawing color and the inside in the other color; "Fill" the outline and the region in the drawing color. A polygon with fewer than three distinct corners has no inside.

### Pending
- REQ-0011-09 From the first press until it is put down or dropped, a curve or polygon is pending: it is shown over the picture as it will be drawn, the pixels shown being exactly the pixels put down (a polygon not yet closed shows its sides as Lines, with no fill and no closing side, whatever the fill style), the picture is unchanged, and it waits for its next stage with no time limit; the app runs no timer for it.
- REQ-0011-10 The colors shall be those of REQ-0010-06 for the button of the first press, kept until the curve or polygon is put down, even if the colors are changed meanwhile. Every later stage uses the same button.
- REQ-0011-11 If the other button is pressed in the canvas area, while a stage's button is held or while the curve or polygon waits, or the user presses Esc or chooses Undo, the app shall drop it: nothing is drawn and no step is added; that Undo undoes nothing else, and the press of the other button and any release after the drop draw nothing (as REQ-0010-08; after an Undo, REQ-0002-09 applies until the button is released). Dropping leaves the history as it was, its redo steps included. While a curve or polygon is pending, Undo is available even if there is nothing to undo, and Redo is not (this amends REQ-0002-06 and REQ-0002-07). A press of the middle or another button does nothing and keeps it pending.
- REQ-0011-12 (Pointer note, Print, 2026-11-03: Print… puts it down too, and stops if that fails, REQ-0026-12.) While a curve or polygon waits (no button held), the app shall put it down as shown (a polygon closed first, REQ-0011-07), then carry out the action, when the user chooses another tool (choosing the active tool again keeps it pending), presses Enter, chooses Save, Save As, New, Open or Quit, chooses an Image action, starts a resize drag with a primary press on a handle (a secondary press on a handle does nothing, REQ-0004-16, and keeps it pending), or does anything else that changes the picture or its file. It stays put down if a prompt or file dialog of that action is then cancelled; Quit with a picture not modified before then shows the unsaved-changes prompt, since the picture is now modified. Putting it down discards the redo steps, as any edit does (REQ-0002-05). Choosing a width or a fill style, or Numpad + or −, changes the pending one at once and it is put down that way later. View actions, scrolling and zooming keep it pending and shown, and so does the window losing focus. While a dialog, the quit prompt or the color chooser is open, Esc and Enter act on it, and presses on the canvas are ignored (REQ-0010-17); the curve or polygon stays pending. (Pointer note, Clipboard, 2026-10-22: Paste and Paste From… apply or drop a pending curve or polygon as a tool change does, but not when the oversize prompt or the file dialog is cancelled, REQ-0016-05, -07, -08.)
- REQ-0011-13 While a stage's button is held, the rules of a shape drag shall apply (REQ-0010-08, REQ-0010-21: other tools, widths, fill styles, View and Image actions ignored; the stage follows the pointer outside the area), and Enter does nothing. If the release never arrives (REQ-0007-18) or the window loses focus (REQ-0010-19), the stage ends as last shown, as if released there; when it is a press that shows the release was lost, that press then acts as a press of its own (the next stage, a corner, or the other button's drop). If Save or Quit is requested, the stage ends as last shown, the curve or polygon is put down, and the app proceeds (REQ-0010-18). A release with no stage held does nothing.
- REQ-0011-14 Putting a curve or polygon down shall be one undo step and mark the picture modified; one that changes no pixel adds no step and no mark (REQ-0010-07). Only the pixels inside the picture are drawn, exactly in the color, with no blending and no antialiasing (REQ-0010-06).
- REQ-0011-15 A first press with Curve or Polygon is ignored when a press of a shape tool is (REQ-0010-17). If the memory to show or put down a curve or polygon cannot be had, the app shall show the dialog "Could Not Draw the Shape" of REQ-0010-20 when the button is released (at once if none is held), drop the curve or polygon and change nothing; a step larger than the whole budget is kept as the only step (REQ-0002-17).

## Edge cases
- A curve whose line is a click (start and end the same pixel): the bends make a loop from that pixel.
- A polygon closed after one side (two corners): the side and the closing side run the same way and back, so where the Pencil's line is not the same both ways (REQ-0001-20's tie-breaking) both show: a side from (10, 10) to (1510, 400) gives, in the picture, 290 pixels one way and 296 with the way back; no inside.
- Corners on the same pixel twice in a row, a side of length 0: allowed; they add no pixel.
- A polygon or curve partly or wholly outside the picture: only the pixels inside are drawn; wholly outside adds no step.
- A double-click whose second click is outside the double-click distance: two corners, no close.
- Many corners (hundreds): no limit but memory (REQ-0011-15).
- A curve put down after only its line (by a tool change or Enter): the Line; after one bend: the curve with both control points there.
- Choosing Polygon while a curve waits, or Curve while a polygon waits: a tool change, so it is put down.

## Plan
### Approach
The core's figure (0010) grows two kinds, so the preview and the drawing stay one function: `EASEL_FIGURE_CURVE` (start,
two control points, end) and `EASEL_FIGURE_POLYGON` (a list of corners, open while pending, closed when put down). Their
outline is a set of pixel paths stamped with the square pen: the curve's one path (REQ-0011-05: the Line in the straight
case, else the sampled, Pencil-joined, thinned path), the polygon's one Line per side. A row of such a figure is no longer
one run, so `easel_figure_row` gives as many runs as the row has (outline runs merged, inside runs between them), and the
caller sizes its buffer with `easel_figure_max_runs`. The polygon's region is computed per row from the corners by the
crossing rule of REQ-0011-08, with no table; the outline comes from a per-row table of x ranges built by
`easel_figure_prepare`, as the Line's is. In the canvas a curve or polygon is a pending gesture: its points, button and
colors live in the canvas from the first press; while a stage's button is held it counts as a stroke (so the shape-drag
rules of 0010 apply unchanged), while it waits it does not. The canvas prepares the pending figure for each frame it
shows, as 0010 does for a shape being dragged, and again when it is put down, from the same points, width and style, so
what is shown is what is drawn (REQ-0011-09); T013 measured a frame within the budget, so nothing is kept between frames. Putting down and dropping are two canvas calls the
window makes at each site of REQ-0011-11 and -12; the window also owns Enter, Esc, Undo, Redo and their sensitivity.

### Files
- `src/core/figure.{c,h}`: the two kinds, the curve's path, the polygon's region, multi-run rows, `easel_figure_max_runs`;
  the apply walks all runs. Tests: `tests/core/test-figure-curve.c`, `tests/core/test-figure-polygon.c` and
  `tests/core/test-figure-step.c` (new: the step, the refusal and the memory held for a curve and a polygon).
- `src/ui/tool.{c,h}` (Curve, Polygon), `src/ui/window.ui` (two toolbox buttons), icons
  `easel-tool-{curve,polygon}-symbolic.svg` and `easel.gresource.xml`, `src/ui/tool-options.c` (Curve on the widths page,
  Polygon on the shapes page).
- `src/ui/canvas.{c,h}`: the pending state, the stages, the double-click and the close near the first corner, the preview of
  the prepared figure, `easel_canvas_put_down`, `easel_canvas_drop`, `easel_canvas_is_pending`, a `pending-changed` signal,
  `easel_canvas_press_timed` (the press with the event's time in ms, which `on_event` calls and the tests call).
- `src/ui/window.c`: hints; the put-down sites; Enter and Esc in the key controller (not while a dialog is open); Undo drops
  and Undo and Redo sensitivity follow `pending-changed`; the width and fill-style actions also apply while waiting.
- Tests: `tests/ui/test-window.c` (a 0011 block), `tests/perf/perf-0011.c`, `tests/mutants.txt`.

### Key interfaces
- `EaselFigure` gains `const int *pts; size_t n_pts; bool closed;` (pairs x, y; for the curve the four points, for the
  polygon the corners); `x0..y1` keep their 0010 meaning for the other kinds.
- `size_t easel_figure_max_runs (const EaselFigure *f)`: an upper bound of the runs of one row (2 × the sides or path
  pixels + 1); `size_t easel_figure_row (const EaselFigure *f, int y, EaselFigureRun *runs, size_t max)` as before.
- Canvas: `void easel_canvas_press_timed (EaselCanvas *, guint button, double x, double y, guint32 time_ms)`;
  `easel_canvas_press` calls it with the monotonic clock. `gboolean easel_canvas_is_pending (EaselCanvas *)` (waiting or
  held); `void easel_canvas_put_down (EaselCanvas *)` (ends a held stage as shown, closes a polygon, applies one step);
  `void easel_canvas_drop (EaselCanvas *)`; `gboolean easel_canvas_enter (EaselCanvas *)` for Enter (puts a waiting shape down,
  does nothing while held, TRUE if one is pending so the key is taken). The double-click time and distance are read from `GtkSettings` (`gtk-double-click-time`, `-distance`) at each press.

### Where it is put down or dropped (every site, REQ-0011-11, -12, -13)
Put down: `change_tool_state` (another tool; the same tool keeps it), `file_action_allowed` (New, Open, Save and Save As:
every way into `save ()` goes through it), `easel_window_close_request` (Quit, the window's close), `attributes_activated` (the
only Image action so far; later Image actions call the same), the canvas's primary press on a resize handle, Enter.
Dropped: Esc, `undo_activated`, the other button's press in the canvas. Not touched: `easel_canvas_end_stroke`, which
`reset_pointer` (focus loss) and `buttons_released` (lost release) also call, so focus loss and a lost release only end the
held stage; the window's put-down sites call `easel_canvas_put_down` after it.

### How each rule is kept
- **REQ-0011-01 to -03:** as 0010's tools: toolbox, options page by tool, hints, crosshair.
- **REQ-0011-04, -05, -06, -08:** core functions, tested against brute force (the curve against a direct implementation of
  REQ-0011-05's steps in the test; the polygon against every pixel center classified by the crossing rule).
- **REQ-0011-07:** the canvas keeps the last click's time, place and button; a press within the double-click time and
  distance is the second click; the close-near-start test uses the release's pointer position in widget coordinates against
  the first corner's center on screen; a press within the double-click time and distance of a click that put the polygon
  down is swallowed with its release.
- **REQ-0011-09, -10, -14:** the prepared pending figure; colors taken at the first press; the apply of 0010 (one step,
  shrunk log, no step when nothing changes).
- **REQ-0011-11 to -13, -15:** the sites above; `is_stroking` true only while a stage is held; Undo and Redo sensitivity from
  the history and `is_pending`; Esc and Enter return `GDK_EVENT_PROPAGATE` while a dialog is open; the memory dialog of 0010
  (`shape-failed`) when a prepare or the apply fails, at the release or at once.

### What grows
Per pending polygon: two ints per corner, copied when a corner is added (doubling, not handed to the history). Per prepare:
a table of the outline's x ranges for the rows the figure spans (for the polygon the placed sides; the side being dragged
has its own Line table, rebuilt per move, so a move costs one side, not all). The step: 12 bytes a changed pixel plus 64,
the log shrunk before the push (0010 T012).

### Measurements
`docs/engineering.md` budgets a pointer event within a frame. A task runs `perf-0011`: a bend of a curve dragged across an
8000 × 8000 picture, and the side of a polygon with 200 placed corners dragged, at 100 % and the fit zoom, to the painted
frame; the put-down of a filled 200-corner polygon over the whole picture (time and peak memory). Fallback if a move is
over a frame: keep the placed sides' table between moves (already the plan) and sample the region's rows as the preview
of 0010 does.

Measured at T013 (`tests/perf/perf-0011.c`, `meson test --benchmark`; 30 pointer moves each, to the next painted frame; the
display is vsync-bound, so a frame costs about 16.6 ms and that is the floor; run with another Easel window open, JOD's from
manual M2, which may have added to the worst cases):

| Drag | 1920 x 1080, 100 % | 1920 x 1080, fit (69 %) | 8000 x 8000, 100 % | 8000 x 8000, fit (10 %) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| curve, second bend | median 16.6, worst **71.6** ms | 16.6, 17.2 | 16.7, 18.0 | 16.6, 17.8 |
| polygon of 200 corners, a side | 16.6, 19.1 | 16.6, 17.9 | 16.7, 17.6 | 16.7, 18.0 |

- Every median is one frame. The first run had one worst case over a frame, the curve's second bend at 1920 x 1080 and
  100 %, 71.6 ms once in 30 moves (the run's first measurement, with another Easel window open). Measured again at the
  check with no other Easel window open, three runs of 30 moves each: the same row gave medians 16.7, 16.7 and 16.6 ms and
  worst cases 21.4, 20.1 and 22.0 ms, so the 71.6 ms did not come back; one other worst case over a frame turned up, 67.7 ms
  once in 30 at 8000 x 8000 and the fit zoom (run 2, the other two runs 21.0 and 18.5 ms), a like outlier to 0010's 85 ms
  at that size and zoom. Reported to JOD, not fixed (no optimization without his word, rule P5).
- The put-down of a filled 200-corner polygon over the whole 8000 x 8000 picture (about 40 million pixels): 172 to 188 ms for
  the call to return and 219 to 254 ms to the next painted frame, in four runs; peak memory 901 MB (365 MB before it: the picture is 256 MB and the undo step
  about 480 MB, held as counted since the log is shrunk before the push).

### Constitution check
Layering holds (geometry and region in the libc-only core; the canvas turns input into core calls); no threads or caches
beyond the prepared figure, which is the one the plan of 0010 already keeps per frame, now per change (P5: measured in
`perf-0011`); hard-edged pixels (P4); one undo step per curve or polygon (design.md, Pending state).

### Risks
- **Double-click detection:** GTK 4 sends no double-click event type; the canvas counts clicks itself from the event time and
  `GtkSettings`. A ui test drives `easel_canvas_press_timed` with chosen times; manual M2 checks a real double-click.
- **Enter and Esc while a dialog is open:** the window's key controller runs in the capture phase, before an `AdwDialog`
  inside the window sees the key; the handlers must let the key through when `dialog_is_open`. Tested in AS-12.
- **Enter with a toolbox button focused:** the capture-phase handler stops Enter while a curve or polygon waits, so a
  focused button is not also activated. Checked in AS-6.
- **Undo with an empty history:** the action is disabled, so Ctrl+Z would not fire; the sensitivity must follow
  `pending-changed`. Tested in AS-9.
- **The preview equals the drawing:** one prepared figure for both; a ui test renders the window during a pending polygon
  and after it is put down (opaque and half-transparent, at 100 % and a scrolled 400 %), as 0010 T007 did.
- **A row with many runs:** a polygon of n sides crosses a row at most n times; the bound of `easel_figure_max_runs` is
  checked by the brute-force tests with polygons of up to 40 corners.
